# Improve the program that made the decision

Stdin is the Gödel run context, not a new mathematical assignment. Pick one consequential decision in the completed or failed attempt. Reconstruct what the worker knew at the time from the run record, its Yah session, and the child sessions relevant to that decision. Use the launch source commit for the program and prompt that actually ran; compare the current accepted revision separately to see what landed. Where did the worker choose well? Did it overlook a decisive experiment, delegate a vague question, repeat a settled check, or promote a heuristic to a conclusion? Distinguish what you observed from what you cannot reconstruct.

Before editing, finish this sentence concretely: “At decision X, this change should cause the program to do Y instead of Z, because ...” Start with the smallest change that explains the difference. You can revise the work or improvement prompt, a child brief, how evidence gets handed forward, the choice of tool, or the Go node graph itself. You are not required to preserve the starter graph. You are also not required to edit it. An argument failing for a sound mathematical reason can be a successful run. Do not optimize away the check that found the failure. Keep the Alpöge exclusion in force in experiments and delegated work.

Examples to consult at the relevant revision, not stages to copy:

- `cmd/godel/README.md` explains how to match a run to its launch source and actual prompt, how the next launch works, and how to check an exact proof candidate. `cmd/godel/mission_nodes.go` shows a work tree and an independently editable improvement tree; its manuscript mission is not yours.
- `.godel/experiments/strategic-retarget/README.md` replays real decisions with only contemporaneous evidence. One extra review made a recommendation too strong. Read the control and the decision not to install that trigger, not just the winning example.
- `.godel/experiments/fixed-statement-review/README.md` shows why a reviewer should see a fixed statement rather than build a moving writer workspace. Compare what evidence the reviewer actually needed with what the assignment supplied.
- `cmd/ff/graph.go` shows one way to route proposals, critiques, and repairs; `cmd/ff/README.md` gives its invocation and cost. `cmd/conjecture/prompts.go` gives examples of briefs for fresh research contexts. Borrow a mechanism when you can name the decision it improves, not because the code already exists.

When practical, replay the old and proposed behaviors on the same pinned input before promoting a change. Give fresh Yah agents enough context to act: The pre-decision evidence they may inspect, the decision to make, what to test with their tools, and the evidence to return. Keep later outcomes and the favored answer out of their briefs. Decide in advance what would distinguish improvement, regression, and a tie. For instance, a better brief might elicit a test on the whole admissible space where the old brief elicited another finite sample. Check what the new test really establishes. A replay establishes at most a different decision; a subsequent live run is needed to assess its mathematical value.

Judge success by a premise closed, an invalid route abandoned for a verified reason, a better justified next experiment, or repeated work avoided without losing a necessary check. Time and inference cost matter when comparing equally useful outcomes. More calls, edits, or confident reports are not mathematical progress. Record mixed and null results, including what would lead you to revert a change.

Use the campaign workflow for changes and run relevant tests on the exact candidate before adoption. Editing a workspace is not adoption; adoption is not a live trial. Leave a short finding with consequential evidence, the decision, and what remains unverified in `.godel/<your name>/` or beside the affected artifact. Only Work's stdout becomes the next cycle's stdin. If you landed a result after Work finished, put its path and remaining scope where the next worker can find it; your final response alone is not a handoff.
