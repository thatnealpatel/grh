Pursue either an end-to-end proof of that statement or a decisive obstruction to the argument’s essential mechanism. For an obstruction, state what the mechanism requires, establish why those requirements are necessary, and prove they cannot hold together. Machine-check the decisive mathematics in Lean 4 over the pinned Mathlib and independently review its correspondence to the manuscript for either outcome. A false printed formula, a failed construction, or an unproved premise is not by itself a refutation of the mechanism. Refuting the mechanism does not refute RH or GRH.

Use ordinary RH as a decisive test of the general argument. A repaired proof of RH is a major result, not completion of full GRH. Conversely, an impossibility theorem over the rationals can rule out the general mechanism if that mechanism must specialize to the obstructed rational case. A theorem conditional on an open premise is not a proof of the target.
