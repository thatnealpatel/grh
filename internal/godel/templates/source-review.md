Review one passage. Keep its durable, human-reviewable handoff in the target repository’s version-controlled `.godel/`, separately from mathematical declarations. Keep transient journals, leases, full-page renders, and the digest-checked PDF cache outside tracked history.

- **Anchor:** Identify the edition by URL and SHA-256. Record the printed pages, their one-based PDF ordinals, and the section, theorem, or nearby text that locates the passage.
- **Reading:** Send the rendered full pages to two fresh image-capable readers. Each reads from the pixels with the same locator and question, without a proposed transcription or the other reader’s answer. Extracted text is a locator, not formula evidence. Check binders, glyphs, and side conditions against the rendered pages.
- **Attribution:** Separate the author’s claim, any result the author cites, and any bridge the project proposes. Do not fill a gap in the reading with the bridge.
- **Boundary:** State the scope of the source passage and what it does not establish. For the final algebraic trace assembly, keep the conditional assembly of the diagonal identities under the theorem’s hypotheses separate from the analytic work that produces those identities and any assumptions needed to realize them in a formalization.
- **Disposition:** A verified reading needs agreement on the in-scope claim and attribution. A disagreement or genuine uncertainty in the source is ambiguous. Only report absence within the rendered passage when both readers find the requested statement missing there. If that blocks translation, record the block; do not emit a theorem or call it a counterexample. A verified reading is not a proof. If the readings use different wording, check equivalence and fidelity with a fresh image-attached adjudication; a changed sign, quantifier, hypothesis or attribution is not a paraphrase. Corroborate both the heading and the visible end of the passage before recording absence. Missing or unreadable images, incomplete responses, and tool or transport failures are errors, not source findings.
