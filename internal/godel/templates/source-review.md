Review one passage. Keep Gödel-owned working files under the target repo’s `.godel/`; the paths below it and the file formats are up to the workflow. Keep PDF binaries, crops, and run journals out of proof history.

- **Anchor:** Identify the edition by URL and SHA-256, then the printed page and the section, theorem, or nearby text that locates the passage.
- **Reading:** State what the passage says. Check binders, glyphs, and side conditions against the rendered page, independently of the proposed transcription. Extracted text is a locator, not formula evidence.
- **Attribution:** Separate the author’s claim, any result the author cites, and any bridge we propose. Do not fill a gap in the reading with the bridge.
- **Disposition:** Say what is verified, ambiguous, or absent, and why. If a missing statement blocks translation, record that finding for this edition; do not emit a theorem or call it a counterexample. A verified reading is not a proof.
