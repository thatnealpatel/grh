## Toward the trace identity (Theorem 12.5.1.1, pp. 519–520)

The [final scalar comparison](obligations/trace-assembly.json) has a visual reading. It needs both evaluations of the **same** diagonal, on the same tests, with the same measure and limit. The algebra does not certify those inputs.

- **Geometric evaluation:** First justify the integrated kernel decomposition and the orbital tail for the two-sided test class; separately identify the normalized $S$-local cutoff asymptotic with the global $S$ contribution, including the two Mellin corrections. The [Chapter 5 citation check](obligations/trace-polar-free-q.md#what-chapter-5-supplies) separates the existence of flat lifts from the nuclear factorization needed on p. 515. For a one-sided cutoff equal to one near zero, a genuine two-moment real Paley–Wiener input can retain a nonzero second derivative after convolution and truncation, even on tests with both Mellin moments zero. A fixed smooth annulus repairs the range and gives the nuclear lift needed for the fixed-cutoff trace transfer in this model. Neither repair replaces the level projection with a multiplier or identifies the global $S$ contribution. The [geometric handoff](obligations/trace-geometric.md) distinguishes these from the vanishing of the omitted linear local terms. Its [scalar cutoff comparison](obligations/trace-geometric.md#smooth-radial-bands-on-the-extended-domain) extends the norm-one domain radially and controls monotone smooth bands by averaging sharp endpoints. The bound vanishes when the product of the position lower and Fourier upper endpoints tends to zero (also the reverse product if both profiles are annular), and the upper transition ratios tend to one. The [fixed-cutoff quotient kernel](obligations/trace-geometric.md#a-fixed-cutoff-quotient-kernel-and-its-haar-diagonal) gives that scalar sum as its normalized Haar diagonal, with locally uniform unit summation and the additive/multiplicative density conversion. The [Paley-Wiener comparison](obligations/trace-geometric.md#the-fourier-multiplier-is-not-the-invariant-paley-wiener-projection) rules out a literal substitution on the real local model; at $s=0$ a rank-one trace difference survives the joint cutoff limit. The literal extracted orbital indentation in (11.3.2.2) appears to have zero Haar mass. The common diagonal, global $S$-piece identification, and two Mellin subtractions remain open.
