## Toward the trace identity (Theorem 12.5.1.1, pp. 519–520)

The [final scalar comparison](obligations/trace-assembly.json) has a visual reading. It needs both evaluations of the **same** diagonal, on the same tests, with the same measure and limit. The algebra does not certify those inputs.

- **Geometric evaluation:** First justify the integrated kernel decomposition and the orbital tail for the two-sided test class; separately identify the normalized $S$-local cutoff asymptotic with the global $S$ contribution, including the two Mellin corrections. The [geometric handoff](obligations/trace-geometric.md) distinguishes these from the vanishing of the omitted linear local terms. Its [scalar cutoff comparison](obligations/trace-geometric.md#smooth-radial-bands-on-the-extended-domain) extends the norm-one domain radially and controls monotone smooth bands by averaging sharp endpoints. The bound vanishes when the product of the position lower and Fourier upper endpoints tends to zero (also the reverse product if both profiles are annular), and the upper transition ratios tend to one. The [fixed-cutoff quotient kernel](obligations/trace-geometric.md#a-fixed-cutoff-quotient-kernel-and-its-haar-diagonal) gives that scalar sum as its normalized Haar diagonal, with locally uniform unit summation and the additive/multiplicative density conversion. This is a project operator on periodized tests, not an identification with the manuscript's nuclear operator. The [Paley-Wiener comparison](obligations/trace-geometric.md#the-fourier-multiplier-is-not-the-invariant-paley-wiener-projection) rules out a literal substitution on the real local model; at $s=0$ a rank-one trace difference survives the joint cutoff limit. The positive-degree trace comparison, nuclear factorization, and global projection are not supplied by that model. The [repaired scalar asymptotic](obligations/trace-scalar.md) evaluates the normalized constructed diagonal: The joint cutoff gives logarithmically normalized local integrals, but taking the Fourier limit at a fixed annular position cutoff first gives zero on the norm-one-vanishing class. Its norm-preserving quotient comparison matches the raw Haar measures under the hypotheses of Theorem 12.4.1.1, hence also their normalizations, but does not identify a kernel or supply a Mellin trace defect. The [integrated stratum split](obligations/trace-geometric.md#integrating-the-stratum-split-is-not-evaluating-its-parts) is absolutely summable given a common integrable diagonal, but its parts depend on the chosen lift. The literal extracted orbital indentation in (11.3.2.2) appears to have zero Haar mass. The common diagonal, global $S$-piece identification, and two Mellin subtractions remain open.
- **One-prime orbital trace:** The [rational trivial-isotype model](obligations/trace-orbital-q.md) gives genuine global theta lifts and a section-based trace-norm limit for tests on both sides of norm one. It distinguishes the exact Chapter 7 quotient from the Chapter 11 Hilbert quotient, which needs closure of the difference range. In this model the literal cutoff closures (11.1.2.6) coincide, and the Mellin sum (11.3.2.5) gives twisted compact averages rather than the unweighted point samples in (11.3.2.1). The [angular character sum](obligations/trace-orbital-q.md#angular-poisson-summation-at-one-prime) restores point evaluation for $p\notin S$, but retains the half twist. Both the corrected comb and the fixed-character sum still vanish for the Chapter 12 support bound $p>T$. For $S=\{\infty\}$, the [standard integer-ring model of (11.2.1.8)](obligations/trace-orbital-q.md#a-zero-line-descent-obstruction-for-the-ring-transition-projector) fails to preserve the zero-line periodization kernel at every finite level, even on genuine theta lifts. The identification with the global level named on p. 489 remains provisional: The literal auxiliary unit factors in the Chapter 7 extraction can change descent. A specified bounded section gives trace-norm convergence in the manuscript order when the level limit precedes removal of the position cutoff, at $a=0$. The [half-weight comparison](obligations/trace-orbital-q.md#half-weight-untwisting-and-the-iterated-limit-on-a-section) bounds untwisting followed by periodization at fixed compact position support, but not without it, even on genuine lifts. At $a=1/2$ a section of shifted theta modes gives a trace-norm limit in the manuscript order, with the level removed first, to the half-twisted comb. The [coupled limit](obligations/trace-orbital-q.md#a-coupled-level-and-position-limit-at-half-weight) gives trace-norm convergence when the band level grows sufficiently fast as a power of the position cutoff. Half-line support removes the rate restriction on any finite set of modes; the argument does not remove it for the full trace. The [shell cutoff comparison](obligations/trace-orbital-q.md#the-right-cutoff-erases-a-fixed-shell-in-the-literal-chain) shows that the right cutoff in the extracted (11.3.2.3) kills a fixed shell if it uses the same decreasing chain. Exhausting places outside $S'$ instead preserves an admissible rational lift, but its shell diagonal is a lift-dependent scalar multiple of the common diagonal, not the circle trace by normalization alone. Continue this branch when there is an independent identification of the selected shell operator with the orbital quotient; the unrestricted joint limit also remains open. The spectral order check below can run independently.
- **Finite-place support subproblem:** The [single-place norm draft](obligations/single-place-norm.md) and the [idele-class support draft](obligations/finite-place-support.md) give a worker target from norm descent and local discreteness to vanishing of the whole single-place test restriction outside $S$. This applies to the omitted linear local terms in the extracted (12.5.1.2), not to the two-sided orbital trace. Both drafts await elaboration; stage and audit their endpoints together once the worker is available.
- **Spectral evaluation:** The [operator-order check](obligations/trace-spectral.md#which-operator-does-the-series-represent) shows that the projected-output series gives $PA$, not the required $AP$. The [canonical-lift check](obligations/trace-spectral.md#why-the-canonical-lift-norm-bound-fails) disproves the uniform upper bound in (10.4.2.24), already for the rational trivial isotype: Two close critical-line ordinates give close coefficient functionals, forcing large biorthogonal vectors. The [rational singleton repair](obligations/trace-spectral-q.md) uses local finite rank away from ordinates and trace-norm summation to prove $AP$ is trace class and compute its Sobolev-capped trace for $s=2r\ge2$. It gives the cutoff limit in the required order in that case only. At almost every positive width the [rational jet-quotient repair](obligations/trace-spectral-strip-q.md) gives trace class in the required order $AQ$ and controls $(1-Q)AQ$ for the projection $Q$ onto the span closure of the zeta jets. It groups high zeros by a strip product and uses a coercive smoothing multiplier, without any individual lift bound. The [cited totality bridge](obligations/trace-spectral-strip-q.md#source-interface-a-missing-totality-step) reverses the nesting of finite common kernels in the extraction. The [Gaussian-division repair](obligations/trace-theta-density-q.md) proves equality of the weighted theta closure and the common jet kernel at the same full-measure choice of widths, using moment-zero compact log approximations. This transfers $AQ$ to $AP$ on the rational trivial isotype only. For the next spectral-to-geometric step, test whether the normalized fixed-cutoff quotient kernel and the spectral operator have compatible Haar diagonals on that isotype with the same positive width and test; neither density nor the jet trace establishes this compatibility. The [Haar diagonal repair](obligations/trace-spectral.md#from-the-weighted-kernel-to-the-haar-diagonal) requires [genuinely shrinking averaging boxes](obligations/trace-spectral.md#the-averaging-boxes-must-actually-shrink); in the provisional reading, the boxes in the manuscript retain the Archimedean angular subgroup. Use a common repaired diagonal for the two evaluations, or prove compatibility. Exclude strip-boundary zeros only if the same width is available throughout the argument.

## From trace to zeros (Chapters 1, 13, and 14)

Neither a trace on a restricted test class nor the empty [divisor heading](obligations/divisor-heading.json) supplies a divisor theorem. The [trace-to-zeros handoff](obligations/trace-to-zeros.md) distinguishes the two trace statements and two proposed routes. The [fixed-parameter subtraction](obligations/trace-to-zeros.md#fixed-parameter-subtraction-over-the-rationals) repairs the rational one-sided Archimedean and spectral bounds and recovers the shifted logarithmic derivative up to a constant, **conditional** on the rational narrow-strip trace. The conditional conclusion at width $a$ excludes zeros strictly outside that strip; exclusion down to the critical line needs widths tending to zero. This does not supply a multiplicity bound. An independent next task is to specify a positivity criterion on the Chapter 13 test class and test whether its moment conditions leave enough tests to detect off-line zeros or recover orders.
