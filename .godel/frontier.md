## Toward the trace identity (Theorem 12.5.1.1, pp. 519–520)

The [final scalar comparison](obligations/trace-assembly.json) has a visual reading. It needs both evaluations of the **same** diagonal, on the same tests, with the same measure and limit. The algebra does not certify those inputs.

- **Geometric evaluation:** First justify the integrated kernel decomposition and the orbital tail for the two-sided test class; separately identify the normalized $S$-local cutoff asymptotic with the global $S$ contribution, including the two Mellin corrections. The [geometric handoff](obligations/trace-geometric.md) distinguishes these from the vanishing of the omitted linear local terms. Its [scalar cutoff comparison](obligations/trace-geometric.md#smooth-radial-bands-on-the-extended-domain) extends the norm-one domain radially and controls monotone smooth bands by averaging sharp endpoints. The bound vanishes when the product of the position lower and Fourier upper endpoints tends to zero (also the reverse product if both profiles are annular), and the upper transition ratios tend to one. The [fixed-cutoff quotient kernel](obligations/trace-geometric.md#a-fixed-cutoff-quotient-kernel-and-its-haar-diagonal) gives that scalar sum as its normalized Haar diagonal, with locally uniform unit summation and the additive/multiplicative density conversion. This is a project operator on periodized tests, not an identification with the manuscript's nuclear operator. The [Paley-Wiener comparison](obligations/trace-geometric.md#the-fourier-multiplier-is-not-the-invariant-paley-wiener-projection) rules out a literal substitution on the real local model; at $s=0$ a rank-one trace difference survives the joint cutoff limit. The positive-degree trace comparison, nuclear factorization, and global projection are not supplied by that model. Next, independently match the repaired scalar limit and Haar constants to Connes's Theorem 4 and (12.4.1.25)–(12.4.1.28) on p. 519. The comparison to the global kernel, limit, and two Mellin corrections remains open.
- **Finite-place support subproblem:** Prepare norm multiplicativity on ideles and descent through the principal quotient by inspecting the pinned Mathlib source. The [single-place norm draft](obligations/single-place-norm.md) awaits the worker; multiplicativity and descent themselves do not depend on its elaboration. This only supplies a premise of the support argument, not of the two-sided orbital trace evaluation.
- **Spectral evaluation:** The [spectral handoff](obligations/trace-spectral.md#which-operator-does-the-series-represent) shows that the projected-output series gives $PA$, not the required $AP$, under the claimed quotient convergence. Next, check (10.4.2.23)–(10.4.2.34) with the canonical lifts and seek a trace-norm estimate for $(1-P)AP$. Once $AP$ is trace class, the cutoff limit follows. The [Haar diagonal repair](obligations/trace-spectral.md#from-the-weighted-kernel-to-the-haar-diagonal) requires [genuinely shrinking averaging boxes](obligations/trace-spectral.md#the-averaging-boxes-must-actually-shrink); in the provisional reading, the boxes in the manuscript retain the Archimedean angular subgroup. Use a common repaired diagonal for the two evaluations, or prove compatibility. Exclude strip-boundary zeros only if the same width is available throughout the argument.

## From trace to zeros (Chapters 1, 13, and 14)

Neither a trace on a restricted test class nor the empty [divisor heading](obligations/divisor-heading.json) supplies a divisor theorem. The [trace-to-zeros handoff](obligations/trace-to-zeros.md) distinguishes the two trace statements and two proposed routes. An independent task is to check if differencing the §1.4 holomorphic test family at a fixed parameter permits uniform Archimedean bounds **without** changing what one can recover about the logarithmic derivative. Alternatively, specify the inputs a positivity route would need and test them on the Chapter 13 test class. Neither task needs to assume that the trace has been proved.
