# Check the prefix before budgeting overlap

**Correction to the rectangle handoff.** In the actual [shared-complement graph](shared-complement-count.md), $d$ is the longest sorted prime prefix up to $Z$ of the reduced product $dy$. If $D=(d,y)>1$, every prime dividing $D$ must be both at most and at least $P^+(d)$, so $D=p^j$ for that one boundary prime $p\le X$. The original product $m=gdy$ then contains at least $2j$ copies of $p$. For the other product $n$, the [joint repeat factor](joint-radical-gap.md) therefore satisfies

$$ e(nm)=nm/\operatorname{rad}(nm)\ \ge\ D^2/p. $$

This holds row by row, even for $g>1$. On the retained residual at $t=1.11$, $q^{.27}e(nm)<|n-m|/q\le H$. Hence $D<(XHq^{-.27})^{1/2}=q^{.060+o(1)}$. In particular $J_2=(d_2,y_{12},y_{22})$ cannot reach $q^{.080}$. This is not a ceiling on $G_1=(y_{11},y_{21})$, which does not divide the prefix by definition. The [strip and short-determinant results](overlapping-lift-count.md) that use only $G_1$ or $T_1T_2$ are unaffected.

**Before retuning the direction sieve, price the edges.** Over *all* gcds and without a restriction on the gap determinant, the number of original ordered product pairs whose $d$-side has $D\ge Q=q^{.056}$ is at most $q^{1.389+o(1)}$. Indeed $|n-m|/q\le H\le (HX/Q^2)e(nm)$ there. The joint-repeat count with $S=HX/Q^2=q^{.278+o(1)}$ costs $q^{o(1)}\sqrt{NS}=q^{1.389+o(1)}$. By symmetry the same holds on the $c$-side. This pays for these edges themselves, **not** all edges on their columns or all unweighted rectangle counts. Remove them before forming degrees and lists if pursuing the edge objective. The remaining graph is a subgraph on the same rows and columns, so the per-row exclusions and degree-bin argument still apply with the remaining degrees. The large-$S$ edge test at small overlap, notably near $rG=1$, is open. No saving on the whole residual follows.
