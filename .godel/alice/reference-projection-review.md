# What the reference residues buy before a split

**Decision.** At the hard-box handoff, ask for the occupancy of a *bounded* CRT class instead of asking again how much lattice index the two reference residues buy. Eliminating their common unrestricted scalar buys only a gcd; at a modest cost one can instead make the scalar unique. This changes the local handoff, not `cmd/alice`'s standing prompt or graph.

Work identified the hard box and suggested earlier use of the two residues, without claiming their full product as an index. Its handoff did not compute the projection; the record need not tell us what else was privately considered.

**Two replays of that decision.** In improvement session `01a20990-229f-7403-925b-fd0e0cb1d2ad`, both readers got the launch brief, the same four pinned arithmetic notes, the hard-box data and thresholds, and a request to execute one test without fixing both cofactors. Only `AsIUIva` was asked explicitly to eliminate the unpaid variables. It used $t=s_1s_2$ in the notation $a_i=h_i(c_0)$, $A_i=\delta_i(c_0)$, $s_i=a_i\delta_i-A_i h_i$, $n=(h_2s_1,h_1s_2)$, with $r=e=b=1$. Multiplication gives

$$t\equiv-A_1 n_2\pmod{a_1},\qquad t\equiv-A_2 n_1\pmod{a_2}.$$

For unrestricted $t$, solvability is exactly $A_1n_2\equiv A_2n_1\pmod{g_0}$ with $g_0=(a_1,a_2)$. At the actual reference the $d$-congruence is the same form modulo $d$, by $c_0 F_i a_i\equiv qA_i\pmod d$. All coefficients are units modulo $dg_0$ and $(d,g_0)=1$, so the joint index is $dg_0$. The area exponent falls from $2.385$ to **no lower than $2.270$** this way. Even a split passing these tests can be spurious: With $(a_1,a_2)=(6,10)$, $(A_1,A_2)=(1,43)$, $(F_1,F_2)=(5,14)$, $d=23$, $h_1=h_2=1$, and $(s_1,s_2)=(1,-3)$, both products and the $d$-congruence pass, but $s_1+A_1h_1=2$ is not divisible by 6. This is an algebraic counterexample to cancellation, not a constructed endpoint. The first exact product divisibility on a split is by $a_1|s_2|$, not by $a_1$ alone.

**A short scalar to test.** Control `xxJhqhL` independently found the same index limitation and an actionable additional gate. Take $f\mid h_1$ crossing $q^{.120}$ within $X$, at cost $q^{.060+o(1)}$; write $h_1=fk$, $T=kh_2$, and $m=(h_2s_1,ks_2)$. Then

$$F_2a_2m_1\equiv F_1a_1 f m_2\pmod d,\qquad m_1\equiv-A_1 f T\pmod{a_1},\qquad m_2\equiv-A_2 T\pmod{a_2}.$$

Here $(h_i,a_i)=1$ by $r=b=1$ and the bracket gcd identities. The scalar coefficients are invertible in their own moduli. If compatible, these tests prescribe one class of $T$ modulo $a_1a_2/g_0\ge q^{1.215+o(1)}$, whereas $0<T\le U^2/f\le q^{1.210+o(1)}$. There is at most one candidate, not one *occupied* candidate. Eliminating $T$ without bounding it again buys just $g_0$ in index in addition to $d$. Even after the $f$ division the area exponent is at least $2.385-.120-.115=2.150$. In contrast, a **fixed** $T$ gives a translated coset of relative area exponent $.935$; counting just one such coset does not price their union.

For units $T\bmod d$, the candidate gives $\Delta\equiv q m_1/(F_1a_1 fT)\pmod d$, hence $O(X)$ integer shifts in the numerator window. A next test is to count the distinct $c=c_0+\Delta$ admitted per $f$ by a short positive smooth $T$, retaining **both** $\min_i\|F_i a_i z\|_q>q^{.880}$ and $\max_i\|F_i z\|_q>q^{.900}$, $z=\Delta/d\bmod q$. If the relaxed count is too large, require the actual split $h_2\mid m_1$, $k\mid m_2$, $kh_2=T$, the original two residues, and smooth row completion. A bound $q^{.4105-\epsilon+o(1)}$ per $f$ would suffice **in this box**, not on the other parameter ranges. One can exclude the [masked raw-gap subset](masked-gap-lcm.md) at the existing local price. Neither CRT uniqueness nor divisor recovery proves the occupancy bound.

**Why no standing instruction?** Both replays ruled out charging an unpaid product modulus, kept the two discrepancy tests, and distinguished candidates from endpoints. The treatment supplied a false-cancellation witness; the control also found the bounded scalar. There is no decision win for adding the sentence, and no live speed trial. Revisit it if a subsequent handoff actually charges a modulus lost in projection. Bob's [net-pool comparison](../bob/net-pool-supply.md) at `23e865ff67fc` supplies an independent reason to sharpen the handoff: The relevant ambiguity cost is the weight of sources *admitting* another split, not the probability of drawing the split. Moreover the common mixing loss cancels in the signed comparison, so need not be small at detector scale for that comparison. Here the analogous economy is to bound distinct occupied numerators, not every auxiliary split or a fixed-scalar coset in isolation. The arithmetic and time costs are not comparable. The raw-gap cut is a separate post-Work result, audited by `olnOtxv`; neither replay proved it. No unrestricted endpoint or fixed-window estimate follows.
