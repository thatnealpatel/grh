## Next useful test on the same retained list

Keep the forms, support, and outer width fixed per ordered
pair. Let $R$ be the residual after the existing screen and
certified interior charges. For known-pool masks it is the
exterior. For general prescribed supports it also includes the
uncertified interior; a count that considers only the exterior
does not pay that part. No claim of additional hits is needed.

At each occupied ordered denominator pair $(d,e)$ choose one
actual reference minimizing the gcd of its two first output
complements, not the denominator gcd. Break ties once. Write
$b_0$ for its signed gap vector, $G=(d,e)$, and
$k_0=(|b_{0,d}|,|b_{0,e}|)$. Count the signed values
$a(v)=\det(b_0,b(v))/(Gk_0)$ occupied by other inputs in $R$;
call their number $N_{de}$. The reference is for counting,
not a new screen. The [rectangle calculation][rectangles]
makes $a(v)$ integral and recovers only $q^{o(1)}$ inputs per
value: The area fixes the first-output determinant modulo
$F_1$, the minimum gcd ensures the primitive-vector recovery
bound for nonzero determinants, and determinant zero uses
exact ratio recovery. No equal-prefix assumption across
inputs is needed. Thus $t_{de}\le 1+q^{o(1)}N_{de}$ for the
number of residual inputs at the pair.

In a dyadic denominator-gcd bin $G\asymp K$, a **sufficient**,
not necessary, cap is $N_{de}\le q^{.0804+o(1)}\sqrt K$.
There are at most $q^{1.055+o(1)}/\sqrt K$ pairs in the bin,
and subpower-many bins. Summing the one reference and the
recovered inputs gives the parent bound $q^{1.1354+o(1)}$.
Test the second-row short residues on these occupied areas.
For a recovered input's sorted prefix $c$, and $i=d,e$, a
second cofactor $h_i$ fixes the only possible signed gap
$\eta_i\equiv q^{-1}cF_2h_i\pmod i$, $|\eta_i|\le H$, since
$2H<i$. The putative output complement is then fixed by
the row equation. Seek a bound on the union of occupied
areas over the eligible cofactor choices, keeping the same
$c$ in both rows. A bound at fixed cofactors or another
uniqueness statement is not that saving. Report the
uncontrolled term if the union does not give the cap.

If pursuing the all-reference count instead, restrict the
rectangle estimate to $R$. With $B_K$ the uncharged ordered
rectangles in the bin, Cauchy gives a
$q^{.5275+o(1)}K^{-1/4}\sqrt{B_K}$ term, and the charged term
is at most $q^{1.134+o(1)}/\sqrt K$. Thus
$B_K\le q^{1.2158+o(1)}\sqrt K$ in each bin suffices. The old
unweighted $1.217-\epsilon$ budget requires
$\epsilon\ge .0012$ for the current target.
