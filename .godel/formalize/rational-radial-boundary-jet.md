## What is proved, and why it differs from the printed claim

Use the [accepted quotient](rational-radial-hilbert-quotient.md) for \(F=\mathbb Q\), trivial norm-one character, \(\chi=1\), in \(x=\log|g|\). This is the Hilbert completion \(H\) of the all-weight smooth profiles in the actual weighted norm, with quotient \(Q\) realized as the orthogonal complement of the closure of \(e^{x/2}\Theta(f)(\tau(x))\) for represented two-mode radial LF inputs \(f\). The map on original representatives is `rationalRadialQuotientMap hab s (rationalRadialThetaTestRange.mkQ u)`, which equals the Hilbert projection after the ambient inclusion. A test \(u\) is the middle-term profile, not a theta generator; there is **no second half twist**. The [Haar comparison](../../Proofs/Theorems/RationalIdeleClassHaar.lean) under this isotype's log-norm pushforward gives exactly \(dx\). For a zero \(\rho\) in the rational trivial isotype put \(z=\rho-1/2\); the printed jet pairing is then \(\int u(x)x^m e^{zx}\,dx\). The completed factor and finite Euler product in Definition 3.2.1.2 and (3.2.1.2)–(3.2.1.4), p. 123/123, and (3.2.1.7), p. 124/124 specialize to \(\pi^{-\rho/2}\Gamma(\rho/2)\zeta(\rho)\), so multiplicities there coincide with zeta multiplicities for \(0<\Re\rho<1\).

[`Theorems.RationalRadialBoundaryJet`](../../Proofs/Theorems/RationalRadialBoundaryJet.lean), **`Masullo.rationalRadialQuotient_boundaryJet_lt`**, proves a necessary condition on the **actual quotient**. For \(-\alpha\le\alpha\), \(s\ge0\), \(|\Re z|=\alpha\), \(m\in\mathbb N\), if \(F:Q\to\mathbb C\) is a continuous complex-linear map giving the above bilinear moment on the classes of all smooth compactly supported profiles, then \(2m+1<s\). This also applies to a functional purported to give the formula on *all* original profiles. No annihilation hypothesis on LF theta, assumption of quotient saturation, or supposition of a boundary zero is used.

**Proof.** Put \(W(x)=e^{\alpha|x|}(1+x^2)^{s/4}\). Its square equals `logSobolevWeight (-α) α s x`; therefore multiplication by \(W\) on test profiles preserves their ambient norm into \(L^2(dx)\). The proof checks the square-integrability, linearity and norm equality on those same representatives, then extends by density to a linear isometry on \(H\). Pull the proposed functional back along the actual quotient projection and transport it to the image of this isometry. Hahn–Banach extends that functional to all of \(L^2(dx)\), without requiring the isometry to be onto. Every smooth compactly supported \(u\) is an all-weight profile because each smooth exponentially reweighted profile has the same compact-support bound and hence is Schwartz. The extension agrees on its class \(Wu\) with the original pairing. The necessary direction of `Masullo.boundaryLogJet_exists_smooth_extension_iff` applies. In the reciprocal-weight norm the nondecaying tail is comparable to \(\int_1^\infty x^{2m-s}\,dx\), which diverges logarithmically at equality. This transport is an implication for the prescribed quotient; it does not identify the weighted log proxy with the quotient or assert that its full ambient dual descends.

**Consequence and limits.** At a boundary zero of order \(N\), the ambient admissible orders below \(N\) are exactly \(m<N\), \(2m+1<s\), by the [strict log criterion](../../Proofs/Props/BoundaryLogJet.lean). Their number is \(\min\{N,\max(0,\lceil(s-1)/2\rceil)\}\). This is an upper bound for quotient jets by the new theorem, not an exact quotient multiplicity or trace formula. In particular at \(s=1\) even \(m=0\) cannot extend to the quotient while both printed caps would retain it for a boundary zero. The [rational membership witness](../obligations/boundary-vector-membership-q.md) obtains a positive-width boundary zero by **varying the twist**, not by proving an off-critical-line zeta zero. At fixed half twist, central-line zeros are interior for every positive width; no actual boundary zero for that twist and isotype is asserted. The [strip point-evaluation audit](../obligations/boundary-point-evaluation.md) addresses a separate topology assertion. The support restriction on \(\varphi\) does not enter this coefficient obstruction; no convolution operator appears in the theorem.
