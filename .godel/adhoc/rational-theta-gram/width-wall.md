# Width walls in the ordered rational theta trace

**Scope.** This is a paper check for the positive-width replacement model of [ordered inertia](ordered-inertia.md), with half twist, degree four, and the rational trivial compact isotype. It gives a scalar RH criterion and an obstruction to proving it from the shared projection structure alone; it is not a calculation of a manuscript shell.

## The scalar criterion

Use the [fixed pulse](../rational-strip-fixed-pulse.md#exact-matrix-endpoint) $h\in C_c^\infty(I;\mathbb R)$, where $\overline I\subset(0,(\log 2)/2)$, and $M_g(z)=\int e^{zx}g(x)\,dx$. A derivative section uses $u_i=(-\partial_x)^i h$, $0\le i\le n$, and entries $G_a(u_i*u_j)$. Products here are **ordinary** convolutions: $M_{u*v}=M_u M_v$. At the [common good widths](../../obligations/trace-theta-density-q.md#bounds-for-division-by-zeta), the [identified ordered scalar](../../obligations/trace-even-separator-q.md#short-tests-separating-the-rational-global-and-joint-local-traces) is

$$G_a(f)=-\sum_{|\Re w|<a}m_w M_f(w),\qquad w=\rho-1/2,$$

where $\rho$ runs over distinct nontrivial zeta zeros, of orders $m_w$. For every fixed section entry or fixed linear combination, rapid ordinate decay and the multiplicity-weighted zero count give $\sum_w m_w|M_f(w)|<\infty$. Thus width differences for $0<c<a<1/2$ are the mass of $[c,a)$ for the finite atomic measure $-\sum_{|\Re w|>0}m_wM_f(w)\delta_{|\Re w|}$. The strict strip means the scalar value at $b$ is the left limit; the right limit includes the wall. In particular the right-minus-left difference through good widths is

$$D_b(f)=-\sum_{|\Re w|=b}m_w M_f(w).$$

This describes the all-width spectral formula, not a geometric identification at a bad width. Continuity **on the good set alone** is unconditional. The criterion is that every finite section have a continuous extension *from the good set to all* $0<b<1/2$.

If RH holds there are no positive-width atoms. Conversely, fix an occupied wall $b$ and one of its upper-half-plane points $w_0$. At this **fixed** $b$, [selected-axis isolation](../rational-strip-fixed-pulse.md#why-accumulating-shifts-work) (steps 2–4) puts the vector with value $1$ at $w_0$ and zero at every other omitted upper point in the closure of the real derivative span in the multiplicity-weighted omission norm. This uses the omitted set $|\Re w|\ge b$, **including** the wall, and is independent of whether $b$ is good. In that norm the wall quadratic $D_b(u*u)=-2\sum_{\Im w>0,\,|\Re w|=b}m_w\Re(M_u(w)^2)$ is continuous. Conjugation accounts for the factor two; the other reflected upper point is among the coordinates set to zero. The target has value $-2m_{w_0}\ne0$. Hence some **finite** real polynomial $q$ gives a nonzero jump for $u=q(-\partial_x)h$. This is a quadratic combination of a finite section, so at least one of its entries jumps. This uses the fixed-$b$ density and domination in the isolation proof, not domination uniform as $b\downarrow0$.

## Differentiation at a fixed grid only

In the [floor grid](handoff.md#finite-matrices-and-the-correct-limit), fix the band, grid, $f$, and real compact position cutoff $\rho$. Set $T=m_\rho A_f$ with $A_fg=f*g$. At a base width $a_0$, use a real orthonormal basis $e_i$ for this fixed grid span in $H_{a_0}=L^2(e^{2a_0|x|}(1+x^2)^2dx)$, and let $Q$ be its projector there. Hold the functions $e_i$ fixed while varying the metric. For its Gram matrix $G$ and numerator $C_{ij}=\langle e_i,T e_j\rangle_a$, the trace is $\operatorname{tr}(G^{-1}C)$. Each grid column is zero sufficiently far left and $O(e^{-x/2})$ to the right, so the weighted moments can be differentiated near $a_0<1/2$ (the output of $T$ is compact). Writing $X=|x|$, differentiation inserts $2X$ in both matrices. Since $G(a_0)=1$, subtracting the term $\operatorname{tr}(G'C)$ from $\operatorname{tr}(C')$ gives

$$\left.\frac{d}{da}\operatorname{tr}(G^{-1}C)\right|_{a_0}
=2\sum_i\langle (1-Q)X e_i,(1-Q)m_\rho A_f e_i\rangle_{a_0}.$$

Both factors are in the original metric at $a_0$. No derivative is taken through grid refinement, band exhaustion, or position exhaustion.

## A fixed restriction producing a wall

In $H_a=L^2(e^{2a|x|}(1+x^2)^2dx)$ let $\mathcal C_B$ be the **actual** profiles $v_d(x)=e^{x/2}\Theta(d,e^x)$ of even Schwartz $d$ with $d(0)=\widehat d(0)=0$ and $\operatorname{supp}\widehat d\subset[-B,B]$. Write $V_B=\overline{\mathcal C_B}$ and $V=\overline{\bigcup_B\mathcal C_B}$, with projectors $P_B,P$. Fix $0<b<1/2$ and choose $t>0$ so $\Lambda=\{\pm b\pm it\}$ avoids the moment zeros of one genuine real $v_0\in\mathcal C_{B_0}$. Such a profile is obtained by taking nonzero real smooth Fourier data of integral zero supported in $(B_0/2,B_0)$ on the positive axis and extending evenly. The sampling formula in the [handoff](handoff.md#floor-core-at-fixed-band) detects it with just the $n=1$ term on that interval. Its left support bound and $O(e^{-x/2})$ right tail give holomorphic $M_{v_0}$ on $|\Re z|<1/2$, not identically zero by Fourier injectivity. Thus on the two lines one need only avoid isolated zeros when choosing $t$.

For $L=(M_{\cdot}(z))_{z\in\Lambda}$ impose $L=0$ on $\mathcal C_B$ **before closing**. Write $V_B^\Lambda$ and $V^\Lambda$ for the band and cofinal closures. The same conditions are used for every $a$. For $S_s g(x)=g(x-s)$, rescaling the input to $e^{-s/2}d(e^{-s}\cdot)$ sends $\mathcal C_B$ onto $\mathcal C_{Be^{-s}}$, preserving both zero modes, and $M_{S_sg}(z)=e^{zs}M_g(z)$. To build exact cardinal profiles, take $\delta>0$ with the four $e^{\delta z}$ distinct. Interpolate polynomials $Q_z$ by $Q_z(e^{\delta z'})M_{v_0}(z')=\mathbf 1_{z=z'}$. Then $v_z=Q_z(S_\delta)v_0$ lies in $\mathcal C_{B_0}$ and $L(v_z)$ is the $z$ coordinate vector. Conjugate nodes give conjugate profiles, so corrections of real profiles are real.

* **Above $b$:** Cauchy–Schwarz makes $L$ bounded in $H_a$. The cardinal profiles prove independence on $V$. Subtract their linear combination with coefficients the evaluation errors of each approximant. This identifies $V_B^\Lambda=V_B\cap\ker L$ for $B\ge B_0$ and $V^\Lambda=V\cap\ker L$, of complex codimension four in $V$.
* **Below $b$:** For shift length $H>0$ and $s_z=\operatorname{sgn}\Re z$, the genuine profile $r_{z,H}=e^{-zs_zH}S_{s_zH}v_z$ has the same unit data; all the other nodes have been killed **exactly** by the finite shift filter. Translation in $H_a$ costs at most $C(1+H)^2e^{aH}$ by the ratio of the weights, so $\|r_{z,H}\|_a=O((1+H)^2e^{-(b-a)H})$. Fix each genuine approximant first, then correct its four moments with these profiles at sufficiently large $H$. Each corrected approximant still fits in a finite band (the left shift may enlarge the band by $e^H$). Hence the restricted cofinal closure is all of $V$. This is not a fixed-band density assertion.

For $a\ne b$, the nested restricted band projectors converge strongly to $P^\Lambda$, the projector onto $V^\Lambda$. Define $G_a^\Lambda(f)$ in the same order as $G_a(f)$: first send the band radius to infinity at fixed compact position cutoff $\rho_R$, then exhaust position. In the band step pair the projections with the trace-class $m_{\rho_R}A_f$, **retaining the right projection**. The restricted-minus-unrestricted trace is then $-\operatorname{Tr}(m_{\rho_R}A_f F)$ with $F=P-P^\Lambda$. Below $b$ it is zero; above $b$, $F$ has rank four. Exhaust position next: $m_{\rho_R}\to1$ strongly with uniformly bounded norms, so $m_{\rho_R}A_fF\to A_fF$ in trace norm. Translations preserve the cofinal subspaces and $A_f$ acts there by integrating translations. Above $b$, $L_z(A_fg)=M_f(z)L_z(g)$, so $FA_fF$ on $FH_a$ represents the induced operator on $V/V^\Lambda$ in the four evaluation coordinates. Only this diagonal block contributes to $\operatorname{Tr}(A_fF)$ after removing the cutoff. In the same normalization as $G_a$ (no pole added back), the difference at good widths is therefore

$$G_a^\Lambda(f)-G_a(f)=\begin{cases}0,&a<b,\\-\displaystyle\sum_{z\in\Lambda}M_f(z),&a>b.\end{cases}$$

Choose $b$ good to separate the artificial wall from any zeta wall. Since $M_h(z)\ne0$ off the imaginary axis, real polynomial interpolation gives $p=q(-\partial_x)h$ with $M_p=1$ on $\Lambda$ and $M_p(\pm1/2)=0$ (interpolate $q=1/M_h$ and $q=0$, respectively). The pulse is still real, mass zero and supported in $I$. For $p*p$, a quadratic combination in the fixed derivative section, the restricted scalar has right-minus-left jump $-4$. No restricted-space or trace assertion at $a=b$ is needed.

The restriction preserves the genuine two-mode band construction, translation covariance with band rescaling, and reality and reflection of the **full** closures (the latter by Poisson for $V$ and negation symmetry of $\Lambda$ for the kernel). The complement gains at most a finite-rank term, so the trace-class and translated-metric balance arguments of [ordered inertia](ordered-inertia.md#the-aggregate-identity-band-first) still apply at each fixed good width off the wall. The corrections are not uniformly small below $b$: at $a=b-1/H$ the norm on a translated compact interval of nonzero $v_z$ mass alone is $\gtrsim H^2$, by the polynomial weight and $e^{-(b-a)H}=e^{-1}$. Nor need their bands stay bounded. Even a fixed compactly cut-off evaluation tends to zero on $r_{z,H}$ for $a<b$ by its boundedness in $H_a$, while the exact evaluation stays one. Thus these structural facts give no uniform control of localization across a width threshold. This does not show that any particular trace cutoff error fails to be uniform. The restricted model is not the closure of **all** admissible inputs above $b$, and does not preserve the full floor span or its scalar identification. It is not a counterexample for $P$: A geometric continuity proof still needs an estimate using the unrestricted span, with control of the ordered trace as widths approach a wall.
