# Trace formula: Spectral input to the final comparison

**Status: Provisional extraction of the [digest-matched edition](../sources/masullo/README.md), with an independent Yah text-only review of the cited spectral interfaces.** No visual attestation or Lean result. Page numbers are PDF ordinals.

The [final scalar comparison](trace-assembly.json) needs (12.5.1.4), p. 520, for the same smoothed geometric diagonal as the local evaluation. The two references on p. 520 do different jobs:

- **Spectral trace:** Theorem 10.4.2.8, pp. 463–464, compares $\operatorname{Tr}(M_\rho A(\varphi)P_a^{\mathrm{spec}})$ with $\operatorname{Tr}(A_a^{\mathrm{spec}}(\varphi))$ as $\Lambda\to\infty$, with $\varepsilon\Lambda=o(1)$ and the annular cutoff (10.4.2.20). The stated $s\ge 0$ there does not by itself meet $s>1$ in its cited trace formula, Theorem 10.3.2.1 (pp. 453–454), or $s\ge 3$ in the kernel definition. The proof ends with p. 464 n. 8 asking for completion. Check the cutoff error and trace limit, not just nuclearity of the uncut operator.
- **Geometric kernel:** Definition 10.4.3.7, pp. 473–474, subtracts the weighted spectral kernel from $\varphi(xy^{-1})$. An almost-everywhere representative of a product kernel does not determine its values on the diagonal. Section 12.1.1, p. 498, explicitly describes that problem. Theorem 12.1.1.3, pp. 502–503, gives a smoothed diagonal and trace identities for kernels without an interval index; its proof cites Theorem 10.4.3.4, which uses the singleton-strip construction of Definition 10.4.3.2 (pp. 470–471). Compare that proof to Theorem 10.4.3.6 and Definition 10.4.3.7 before applying it to the strip with nonempty interior. In particular, check the weight in the kernel and trace measures and the order of cutoff and convolution. Given the corresponding diagonal identities in that strip, $\varphi(1)=0$ leaves the negative spectral diagonal; the sign is not the hard step.

**Strip endpoints are a distinct premise.** In (10.3.2.3)–(10.3.2.4), p. 454, the sum includes $b=\pm\alpha$ with capped multiplicities, while the p. 520 target includes only interior zeros with their full orders. At $a=1/2$, the parameter $c=\chi|\cdot|_F^{1/2+b+it}$ gives the Mellin argument $c|\cdot|_F^{-1/2}$ and orders agree for $|b|<\alpha$. They do not therefore agree on the entire sum. Justify a choice of strip with no boundary zeros that is compatible with the other evaluation and downstream argument, or explicitly account for the boundary contribution. Taking $\alpha=0$ in the singleton construction does not prove the interior-strip sum.
