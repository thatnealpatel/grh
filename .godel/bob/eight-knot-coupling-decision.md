price within finite slabs while preserving its slab integral.
On split slabs it allowed measurable $A$, releasing $B$ on
the signed and $0,2,4$ split slabs. It used integrated atom
support on unsplit slabs and a sampled pointwise price on the
tail. For the 71 masks with sole negative site $-216$, the
largest computed price was about $-4.2495\cdot10^{-8}$ with
no more $B$ releases and $-7.8504\cdot10^{-9}$ with one. With
two, it was already $+2.2136\cdot10^{-8}$; with four, about
$+6.5367\cdot10^{-8}$. The scout freed the most expensive
slabs in each mask, then took the group maximum. None of
these numbers is a certified bound; the scout priced the
radial tangent, not the full quadratic and modulus
constraints. Against $-10^{-10}$ the first estimated miss
is about $2.2236\cdot10^{-8}$, at two extra releases.
