# The whole rational geometric trace equals the normalized local expression

Over $\mathbb Q$ in the trivial compact isotype, the target is the following for arbitrarily small positive strip half-widths $\alpha$ and a common admissible Sobolev degree: For every $f\in C_c^\infty((0,\infty))$ with $\int f=0$, the **whole** half-twisted geometric trace (band limit first at fixed position cutoff, then position exhaustion) equals the normalized real and finite local terms of (12.5.1.1–2) minus $M_f(1/2)+M_f(-1/2)$, where $M_f(z)=\int e^{zx}f(x)\,dx$. The minus sign applies to both polar terms.

This is the geometric comparison, not the [evaluation of the ordered trace by interior zeros](../closed/rational-radial-ordered-inner-trace.md). One needs both evaluations for the **same operator and widths**. Together with the full explicit formula they would yield the test agreement in [`Masullo.riemannHypothesis_of_strip_test_agreement`](../../../Proofs/Props/ZetaTestSumComparison.lean). Lean checks that implication, not its premise. The [raw-Haar field counterexample](../closed/masullo-raw-haar-global-window-formula.md) does not settle this question: The rational norm-one volume is one.

**Next discriminating test:** Fix a common good width, even degree $s\ge4$, and $f=b'$ with $0\ne b\ge0$ smooth and compactly supported in $(0,\log 2)$. All finite-place scalar samples vanish. The proposed whole trace is therefore $J_f-M_f(-1/2)-M_f(1/2)$, with $J_f$ the normalized real-place term. The [joint local projection calculation](../../obligations/trace-joint-local-q.md) gives $J_f-M_f(-1/2)$ in its own cutoff order. Identify the actual global remainder at fixed cutoffs and compute its limiting trace: It must supply exactly $-M_f(1/2)=\tfrac12 M_b(1/2)>0$ relative to that local limit. The [global handoff](../../obligations/trace-to-zeros.md) records the orders and missing identification. A computation replacing the global piece by the local projection, assuming the orbital tail vanishes, or merely equating cut output ranges does not run this test. One successful bump would be a calibration, not the asserted all-test identity.
