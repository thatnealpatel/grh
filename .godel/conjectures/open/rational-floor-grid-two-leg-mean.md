# Can the two-leg phase mean be summed separately on the rational floor grids?

**Claim tested:** On the nested grids $h_j=2^{-j}$, $m_j=4^j$, take the full metric $w_a(x)=e^{2a|x|}(1+x^2)^2$, $0\le a<1/2$. At fixed smooth nonnegative nonzero compact position cutoff $\rho$, use the [global trace card](rational-normalized-global-geometric-trace.md)'s test $f=b'$ for $0\ne b\ge 0$ smooth and compactly supported in $(0,\log 2)$. On a physical orthonormal basis of each new detail space, write each profile locally as its phase mean times $g(x)=2e^{-x/2}$ plus its centered part. If this split is made on **both** legs of $M_\rho A_f$, do the mean–mean partial sums and the remaining partial sums converge separately as the level grows?
