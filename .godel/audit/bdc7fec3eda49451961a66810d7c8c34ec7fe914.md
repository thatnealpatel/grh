Accepted parent `beec9711ce91e866712d93df734f572d220deac1`, reviewed commit `bdc7fec3eda49451961a66810d7c8c34ec7fe914`.

This handoff records defects, not a proof repair or a verdict on RH/GRH.

## Finding

1. The review boundary can silently discard a record and mark its chapter covered. A zero-exit reply with chapter set to chapter-01, complete set to true, and a populated record under the misspelled key recrod passed cmd/walk/traversal.go:133–144. json.Unmarshal ignored the unknown key, leaving Record nil. Lines 163–190 then treated the reply as a legitimate completion without a final record. The runner probe observed chapter-01.json with complete:true, cursor:null, and records:[], followed by a chapter-02 request. This decoder behavior predates the target, but the new built-in model reviewer still uses it despite being directed to follow the exact schema. Syntax retry does not protect coverage from this failure.

2. The new launch recipe does not establish which accepted bookmark the reviewer will use. .godel/walk/README.md:12–20 supplies GRH_LAUNCH_ROOT, and cmd/walk/main.go:38–46 and cmd/walk/reviewer.go:10–19 use it as the reviewer cwd without selecting a campaign target. In installed Yah v0.0.59, internal/yah/yah.go:825–829 and 1323–1341 select the target from YAH_CAMPAIGN_TARGET, a valid suggestion, or a sole local bookmark. A fresh headless reviewer in a detached durable workspace with two local bookmarks and no explicit target fails before the first review, even when the documented root is correct. With a checked-out bookmark different from the owner's intended target, it can instead consult the wrong accepted history. This result follows from the installed selector's source; the stub runner did not exercise live campaign selection.

## Proposed repair or follow-up

1. Reject unknown review fields before applyReview while keeping syntax errors distinct from schema errors. Add a regression case for complete:true with recrod and assert that it neither retries nor publishes nor schedules the next chapter.

2. Make the intended local bookmark explicit in .godel/walk/README.md, following cmd/godel/README.md:8, and pass or require YAH_CAMPAIGN_TARGET before launching the reviewer. Do not infer the owning campaign's target from the workspace path.
