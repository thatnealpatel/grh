## Finding

1. cmd/audit/history.go:67–85 treats two sequential ref samples as a simultaneous snapshot. Even an ordinary linear advance that keeps jj and Git in agreement can fall between those samples. cmd/audit/audit.go:77–84 and 105–118 propagate the false disagreement as a fatal error. A valid completed review can therefore be discarded and unattended coverage stops until restart. The failure is fail-closed, not a false grant of coverage.

2. The continuous loop in cmd/audit/main.go:119–121 retains one additional waiting Go process per normally completed pass. Gödel v0.0.5 run.go:122–125 and launch.go:14–19,60–61 exec `go run .`, but Go 1.27.4's `buildRunProgram` calls `base.RunStdin`, which starts and waits for a child. The child becomes the next runner without releasing its parent. Thus the documented unattended workflow accumulates live processes even with no lingering Yah work. It can eventually hit a process or memory limit despite every review succeeding.
