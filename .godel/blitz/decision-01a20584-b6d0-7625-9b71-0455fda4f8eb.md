## Classical RH for the Riemann zeta function

The target is Mathlib's `RiemannHypothesis` for `riemannZeta`.
`Proofs/Theorems/RationalIdeleRadialExplicitFormula.lean:61–75` gives
an unconditional, absolutely convergent full-order zero sum with both
polar Mellin moments and normalized real and finite terms on actual
rational idele-class Haar. It is not an operator trace.
`Proofs/Theorems/RationalRadialStripTrace.lean:66–88,102–160` shows that
substituting an open-strip sum is valid at a positive width exactly
when no zeta zero is omitted, including at the boundary. The scalar
identity at all positive widths is equivalent to RH. The weaker
sufficient premise in `Proofs/Props/ZetaTestSumComparison.lean:25–39,78–110`
is agreement on every smooth compact positive-log mass-zero test with
one order-dominated, closed-strip-supported weight family per width,
for arbitrarily small positive widths. That agreement is not proved.

`Proofs/Theorems/RationalFixedAnnulusTrace.lean:17–41` computes a local
projected trace. `Proofs/Theorems/RationalThetaRadialCut.lean:171–215,291–313`
(the finite-prime closure landing `90b37feac3f2c0c9a1056e768f6187e50f452824`)
controls cut ranges at width zero, not an operator identity at positive
width. `Proofs/Theorems/RationalOrbitalObstruction.lean:25–35` refutes the
literal double-section Haar weight, zero versus the unshifted prime-two
value $\log 2$. Independently, the same-chain right mask erases the fixed
shell, as checked on the rendered PDF pp. 495–496, (11.3.2.3–4), in
`.godel/obligations/trace-orbital-q.md:243–325`. This audit does not
verify the intended Haar indentation in (11.3.2.2), which remains
provisional. Neither obstruction rejects all shell-preserving lifts.

The missing comparison is between the actual global $S$-piece with
its terminal right projection and the normalized local evaluation
minus both Mellin moments, after independently accounting for the
orbital term. The identification at PDF p. 494, (11.3.1.4), and
pp. 517–519, Thm. 12.4.1.1, remains provisional. The rendered record
`.godel/obligations/trace-assembly.json` verifies the concluding
algebra at PDF pp. 519–520, Thm. 12.5.1.1, not the analytic evaluations.
The specified joint local projection in the control at
`.godel/obligations/trace-to-zeros.md:84–142` supplies the $-1/2$ moment
but not the $+1/2$ moment. For the derivative of a nonzero nonnegative
smooth bump supported in $(0,\log 2)$, the finite linear samples
vanish, but the $+1/2$ moment is nonzero, and there is no deduction of
orbital vanishing. Main's literal-lift check stopped before this bump
calculation. There is no independently specified global input on
which to run it.

**Reopen RH** when a specified Haar-compatible lift recovers the
actual global kernel at one admissible positive width, with a
shell-preserving terminal right projection and compatible cutoffs.
Compute the orbital and normalized local contributions on the short
bump independently, keeping both Mellin subtractions explicit and
taking the level limit at fixed position support before position
exhaustion. A match would justify broader tests, not prove RH.

## Full Masullo GRH

The target is $\Re s=1/2$ for every nontrivial Hecke zero of every
continuous unitary idele-class character of every number field,
including ramified characters. This scope is inherited from the
rendered review in `.godel/blitz/decision-01a202cd-7acf-7edb-97ce-044d9614d43e.md:78–87`,
PDF p. 6, Acknowledgements, and p. 528, §14.1.2. The multiplicity bound
and the higher-rank conjecture are separate. The two-reader rendered
record `.godel/obligations/divisor-heading.json` finds no statement at
PDF pp. 522–523, Thm. 13.3.1.2. The restricted Mellin jets and capped
critical-line orders of the preceding theorem, p. 522, (13.3.1.1–3),
do not give full-order agreement. The abstract zero-mass uniqueness
argument needs separated, quadratically summable divisor differences
and agreement on every admissible mass-zero test, not scalar jet
checks on a distinct quadratic class character.

The first two conductor-three blitzes supplied common horizontal
heights (`56db126833c651350062b7de70fcc8a8e5ee1f5a`), finite residues,
and full-order exhaustion (`0ec16aa411ec0b6bda96791e666ded761640c801`).
`Proofs/Theorems/DirichletThreeResidueExhaustion.lean:88–102` equates the
absolutely convergent full-order sum for Mathlib's unscaled odd
primitive quadratic completion modulo 3 to the upward right-minus-left
contour on smooth compact log tests, without poles. It locates no zeros.
The third attempt, `attempt-01a20554-f6c7-7737-b4a7-25b8aef112fd-ZNGfbe.md`,
landed as a note only at `c6ba655a2bd4172c960654957aa0f0f4a2f8ad26`.
The repaired idelic pairing equals that exact unscaled completion in
reviewed ordinary mathematics, with neither an extra scalar nor a
conductor power. For $g(0)=1$, subtracting the upward left leg gives
$+\log 3$ after reflection. Multiplication by $3^{s/2}$ instead adds
half of that contact to each original leg and leaves the difference
unchanged. The note attributes rendered readings of PDF p. 152,
Def. 3.4.3.2, and pp. 614–615, (B.4.2.2–4). Both derivative signs in
(B.4.2.3) are reversed; the bridge uses repaired theta inversion.
No Lean bridge or strip-supported evaluation landed.

The general Gaussian continuation at `5401cebe488cc9a65902161f0054ca7c0e223c70`
and strip bound at `bd041db358941d519fccde5c2c734aca4dc193ba`
(`Proofs/Theorems/IdeleClassGaussianContinuation.lean:65–106`) identify
the exact completed Euler product above one under the stated Borel and
class-representative hypotheses. They use a norm-one-averaged corrected
Mellin integral with explicit possible simple poles, entireness for
nontrivial norm-one restriction, and quadratic strip growth of the
same pole-cleared numerator. This is not a general normalized functional
equation, logarithmic-derivative estimate, or contour exhaustion.
The general-field normalization mismatch at PDF pp. 519–520,
(12.5.1.1–3), documented in `.godel/obligations/trace-window-q-sqrt-minus-five.md`,
is repaired at the level of the pole coefficient by dividing out the
global Haar volume, not at the level of the trace comparison.

**Reopen full GRH** when the same conductor-three positive-width global
diagonal is specified well enough to evaluate spectrally and geometrically
on matching Mellin tests, with Haar, shell-preserving lift, projection,
cutoff order, and separate conductor, identity, pole, and central-zero
accounting. Readiness requires computable inputs, not prior proof of
agreement. A mismatch would reject that comparison, not either
hypothesis. A match would still leave the all-field, all-character
argument, including the corresponding analytic inputs.

## Why stop here

The pinned Main and Companion evidence concerns adjacent contour and
test-domain work. In particular, `Proofs/Lemmas/CompactLogMellinPeriodization.lean:68,105,139–153`
supplies a smooth compactly supported difference primitive for the
compact-away repair of PDF p. 260, Lemma 5.3.4.2(2), not an adelic lift.
Companion's running record supplies no outcome for positive-ray
transport. Neither a saved input nor a running marker establishes
completion or nonduplication.

The tree advanced during the cycle; the assessment pin did not.
The post-pin Main landing `54fda388fab1f0f948f63d8b8c2fadc828e19b6f`
gives a moving-disk Jensen count for the continued trivial-character
numerator and leaves logarithmic-derivative bounds downstream.
The post-pin remnant and shell notes at
`372d8d613e4834fec4806defd05f37551ec58683` and
`d8d5ac887d3f7bfb36f378330a612808165173d0` separate pointwise mask
limits from lift invariance, shell localization, and the local action
comparison. None specifies the common diagonal needed for either
control. The absence of a runnable comparison at that interface, not
the lack of useful upstream lemmas, is the reason to decline both.
