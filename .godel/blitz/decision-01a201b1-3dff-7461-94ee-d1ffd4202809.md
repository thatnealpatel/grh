## Classical RH

The target is actual Mathlib `RiemannHypothesis` for the Riemann zeta
function. At the pin, `Masullo.rationalIdeleRadial_explicit_formula`
(`Proofs/Theorems/RationalIdeleRadialExplicitFormula.lean:66`) evaluates
the absolutely summable full-order zero sum on smooth compact log tests
against normalized rational idele-class Haar, retaining both polar Mellin
terms and the normalized local terms. This is a checked scalar formula,
not a global operator trace or a certification of the printed identities.
`Masullo.riemannHypothesis_of_strip_test_agreement`
(`Proofs/Props/ZetaTestSumComparison.lean:30,94`) proves RH conditional
on this comparison: Below every positive bound there is a common positive
half-width with one family of natural weights bounded by full orders and
vanishing outside the corresponding closed strip, whose sum agrees with
the full-order sum on **every** smooth compact positive-log test of
integral zero. No zero-width trace or simplicity claim is needed.

RH is the closer target, but the two evaluations of the **same global
Haar diagonal** on common tests at those widths are not established.
The inherited independent rendered review in
`.godel/obligations/trace-assembly.json` checks the final algebra at PDF
pp. 519–520, Thm. 12.5.1.1, not the spectral evaluation, both Mellin
subtractions from the global $S$-piece, the orbital evaluation, or their
common admissibility. The exact shell lift and right orbital cutoff,
level-before-position limit with the terminal projection, and identification
of the local multiplier with the global $S$-piece remain provisional
source interfaces (PDF pp. 488–489, (11.2.1.6–10); pp. 492–496,
(11.3.1.2), (11.3.1.4), and §11.3.2; p. 517, (12.4.1.8–12)). So do the
strip-edge weights and Sobolev test compatibility (PDF pp. 454, 463, and
473, (10.3.2.3–4), Thm. 10.4.2.8, and Def. 10.4.3.7).

On the derivative of a nonzero nonnegative smooth bump supported in
$(0,\log 2)$, the tested joint local replacement gives the $-1/2$ Mellin
subtraction but misses the nonzero $+1/2$ subtraction
(`.godel/obligations/trace-joint-local-q.md` and `trace-to-zeros.md`).
Vanishing linear prime samples does not evaluate the orbitals. The
reviewed ordinary-math Gram calculation in
`.godel/adhoc/rational-theta-gram/handoff.md` keeps the terminal right
projection in an unsplit rational model, but does not identify either
the actual $S$-piece or the orbital cutoff. These checks reject specific
shortcuts, not a possible global repair.

**Reopen RH** when the actual rational global $S$-piece and right orbital
cutoff are identified with the terminal projection intact. At one common
positive width, take the level limit at fixed position support before
exhausting position, and use the short bump derivative to test both
Mellin subtractions and the actual orbital contribution. Success at one
width on that test would justify further work, not prove RH.

## Full Masullo GRH

The target is location on $\Re s=1/2$ of every nontrivial Hecke zero for
every number field and every continuous unitary idele-class character,
not just finite-order characters. The prior evidence reader's rendered
inspection is recorded in `decision-01a20149-6c55-768f-9fd2-b751564c1bca.md`
(PDF p. 6, Acknowledgements, and p. 528, §14.1.2). Noncentral multiplicity
and the broader automorphic conjecture are separate (PDF pp. 528–529,
§14.1.2 and Conj. 14.1.2.1). A rational specialization, a single isotype,
or a conditional statement does not discharge this target.

The accepted actual-character completed Euler product gives nonvanishing,
differentiability, and the split logarithmic derivative on $\Re s>1$
(`Proofs/Theorems/CompletedHeckeEulerProduct.lean:98`), not continuation.
Local finite and Archimedean pairings do not identify the normalized
global integral. The accepted prerequisites added since the previous
assessment are:

- Finite completion compactness and open units at
  `9887313215d9a848b19e90a2649ea3dc80a482b9`
  (`Proofs/Lemmas/FiniteCompletionCompact.lean:85,93,98`).
- Regular restricted-product Haar, exact stage comaps, and Borel
  uniqueness at `b1697abb72e85f1c13e2ddb5794b49f4516d6097`
  (`Proofs/Theorems/IdeleRestrictedProductHaar.lean:42`), given local
  Haar with unit masses one off a finite set, retaining the exceptional
  masses.
- Complex and absolute stage tensor integration at
  `d9aa363bfe76074b8d7e476cea2193379bf04b4b`
  (`Proofs/Theorems/IdeleFiniteStageIntegral.lean:44`), given the
  pointwise tensor pullback, stage measure identification, and local
  integrability.
- Global integrability and both cofinal integral limits at the assessment
  pin (`Proofs/Theorems/IdeleFiniteStageExhaustion.lean:34`), given
  integrable stages and a uniform bound on their absolute masses.

The actual global Mellin density factorization, the uniform Euler bound
for that density, and the discriminant and quotient normalizations are
still needed for the actual normalized global pairing. The main loop's
accepted obligation note `.godel/obligations/hecke-right-line-majorant.md`
maps its next step to the unramified absolute Euler factor for the stage
bound, not to a completed contour. The checked chain still lacks general
continuation and reflection with the arithmetic conductor, zero and pole
data and horizontal bounds for contour exhaustion, and a normalized common
trace or divisor identity. The general measure and contour interfaces
remain provisional source readings (PDF pp. 123–126, §3.2.2 and
Thm. 3.2.2.1; pp. 135–141, (3.3.1.28–32) and (3.3.2.24); p. 560,
(A.3.2.1–5); pp. 614–615, (B.4.2.1–4)). The two independent rendered
readers recorded in `.godel/obligations/divisor-heading.json` found only
a heading and insertion footnote at Thm. 13.3.1.2, not a statement or
proof (PDF pp. 522–523). Thm. 13.3.1.1 (p. 522) concerns a capped
critical-line sum, not a positive-width spectral sum.

**Reopen full GRH** when an accepted general-character completed-contour
interface and an explicit normalized common-diagonal or divisor identity
permit a one-positive-width comparison of full and strip-supported
residue weights on the same admissible ramified-isotype test, with the
central and identity terms accounted for. Success would justify further
work, not prove the full assertion.
