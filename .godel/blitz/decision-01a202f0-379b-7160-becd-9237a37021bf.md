## Classical RH for the Riemann zeta function

The strongest accepted endpoint is the unconditional scalar theorem
`Masullo.rationalIdeleRadial_explicit_formula`
(`Proofs/Theorems/RationalIdeleRadialExplicitFormula.lean:34–49,61–75`,
landing `9330ec2f38fe508354301080f8110dc70989458a`; prior build, replay,
and axiom record `.godel/obligations/rational-explicit-formula.md:1374–1428`).
For smooth compact log profiles, the absolutely summable zero sum uses full
orders and equals the two polar Mellin moments at $s=0,1$ minus the real
and finite local terms, on normalized rational idele-class Haar. The real
term already incorporates half-Haar normalization. This is a scalar
project repair, not an operator trace. Manuscript alignment is provisional
(PDF p. 614, (B.4.2.2); p. 623, (B.4.4.16); p. 625, (B.4.4.20)).

`Masullo.riemannHypothesis_of_strip_test_agreement`
(`Proofs/Props/ZetaTestSumComparison.lean:30–39,77–109`) is conditional.
For arbitrarily small positive half-widths, one weight family per width,
bounded by the true orders and vanishing outside the centered closed strip,
must reproduce the full sums on **every** smooth compactly supported
positive-log test of integral zero. One width for the whole class would
give a strip bound. One test would not.

The inherited two-reader rendered review in
`.godel/obligations/trace-assembly.json` verifies the algebra of combining
two evaluations of the same Haar diagonal and restoring both Mellin terms
(PDF pp. 519–520, Thm. 12.5.1.1, (12.5.1.1–4)), not the evaluations or
their common domain. The review attributed in
`decision-01a20240-39f6-7778-8369-892e00fbbf16.md` found that the printed
limits omit the terminal right projection (PDF pp. 488–489, (11.2.1.4),
(11.2.1.9)). The fixed-position project repair retains it
(`.godel/obligations/trace-hardy-diagonal-q.md:234–257`). The actual global
rational $S$-piece, shell lift, right orbital cutoff, and even one positive
half-width common to the geometry and spectral calculation remain missing.
The decisive geometric and width identifications remain provisional (PDF
pp. 494–496, (11.3.1.4), (11.3.2.3); pp. 517–519, (12.4.1.8–12),
(12.4.1.23–28); p. 454, (10.3.2.3–4); p. 463, Thm. 10.4.2.8; p. 473,
Def. 10.4.3.7).

**Reopen RH** after confirming the diagnostic is unassigned, identifying
those three global objects with the terminal right projection retained,
and establishing one common admissible positive half-width. In the
trivial compact isotype over $\mathbb Q$, compare the strip evaluation
with the actual right orbital term and both Mellin subtractions on $b'$
for a nonzero nonnegative smooth bump $b$ supported in $(0,\log 2)$.

The test has zero integral and no prime samples, but its $+1/2$ Mellin
