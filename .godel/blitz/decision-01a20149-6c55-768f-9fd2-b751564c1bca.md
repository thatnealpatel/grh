## Classical RH

The target is Mathlib `RiemannHypothesis` for `riemannZeta`. The accepted
`Masullo.rationalIdeleRadial_explicit_formula`
(`Proofs/Theorems/RationalIdeleRadialExplicitFormula.lean`) unconditionally
evaluates the absolutely convergent full-order sum for smooth compact
log profiles on normalized rational idele-class Haar. It has both
polar moments and the real and finite local terms. It is a scalar
explicit formula, not a global trace. The accepted
`Masullo.riemannHypothesis_of_strip_test_agreement`
(`Proofs/Props/ZetaTestSumComparison.lean`) concludes actual RH conditional
on this comparison: Below every positive width bound, choose one common
positive half-width and one family of natural weights bounded by full
orders and supported in the closed strip at that width. The weighted
and full sums must then agree for **every** smooth compactly supported
positive-log test of integral zero. Neither a zero-width trace nor a
multiplicity cap is required.

The missing step is to evaluate the same global Haar diagonal
geometrically at common widths on common tests, taking the level limit
before the position exhaustion and retaining the terminal right
projection. The connection to the joint local cutoff, both Mellin
subtractions, and the orbital contribution remains unproved (PDF
pp. 488–489, (11.2.1.6–10); pp. 494–496, (11.3.1.4), §11.3.2;
p. 517, (12.4.1.8–12); pp. 519–520, Thm. 12.5.1.1). The cycle's evidence
reader inspected rendered p. 514 and found only a heading and an
insertion footnote in §12.3. The inherited rendered review in
`.godel/obligations/trace-assembly.json` verifies the final algebra on
p. 520, not the evaluations or their common test and cutoff conditions.
The literal shell/operator and strip-boundary/Sobolev interfaces remain
provisional source readings (PDF pp. 488–489, 494–496, (11.2.1.6–10),
(11.3.1.4), §11.3.2; pp. 454, 463, 473, 519–520, (10.3.2.3–4),
Thm. 10.4.2.8, Def. 10.4.3.7, Thm. 12.5.1.1).

The documented local joint-cutoff replacement in
`.godel/obligations/trace-joint-local-q.md` retains the $-1/2$ Mellin
subtraction but misses the $+1/2$ subtraction on the above-one control.
A derivative of a nonzero nonnegative bump supported in $(0,\log 2)$ has
zero mass and no linear prime samples, yet the $+1/2$ moment is nonzero.
Vanishing of those samples does not show vanishing of the orbital trace.
The ordinary-math rational spectral repairs address a common set of
positive widths on the spectral side, not this geometry. There is no
distinct explicit global operator and cutoff in the assessed evidence on
which to run a new comparison. RH is closer than full GRH, but the
conditional endpoint's premise is still missing.

**Reopen RH** when a distinct explicit global $S$-piece and right orbital
cutoff retain the terminal projection at fixed position support and a
common positive width. Test both Mellin subtractions and the actual
orbital trace on the short bump derivative. One successful comparison
would warrant further work, not establish agreement for the whole test
class at arbitrarily small widths.

## Full Masullo GRH

The target is location on $\Re s=1/2$ of all nontrivial Hecke zeros for
every number field and every continuous unitary idele-class character.
The cycle's evidence reader directly inspected rendered PDF p. 6
(Acknowledgements) and p. 528 (§14.1.2) for that scope and the claimed
location deduction, which includes an unresolved positivity reference.
The noncentral multiplicity bound (pp. 528–529, §14.1.2) and the broader
automorphic conjecture (p. 529, Conj. 14.1.2.1) are separate. The
inherited rendered review in `.godel/obligations/divisor-heading.json`
found no statement or proof under Thm. 13.3.1.2 (PDF pp. 522–523). The
capped critical-line formula in Thm. 13.3.1.1 (p. 522) is not the
positive-width trace. The rational specialization, a single isotype,
and a conditional GRH endpoint do not discharge the target.

At the pin, the accepted finite Euler product, finite right-line pair
with the actual conjugate, and tested scalar Gamma legs are right-line
results. The newer `exists_archimedeanHeckeRestriction_real` and
`exists_archimedeanHeckeRestriction_complex` classify actual completion
restrictions, and `realGaussianZetaDensity_integral` and
`complexGaussianZetaDensity_integral` give convergent scalar Gaussian
pairings for $\Re s>0$. The complex pairing uses the opposite angular
weight. The evidence reader verified the displayed unbarred positive
power at PDF p. 152, (3.4.3.5), but the combined comparison with the
pairing at p. 130, (3.3.1.7), remains provisional. These results do not
identify the global zeta integral. The accepted `IdealClassPrimeFive.lean`
identifies the unique prime above five of norm five and identity ideal
class in the full ring of integers of $\mathbb Q(\sqrt{-5})$. It does not
realize the idelic character or establish the all-prime local identities
needed by `HeckeDirichletFactorization.lean`.
