## Classical RH for the Riemann zeta function

The strongest unconditional input is the scalar repair
`Masullo.rationalIdeleRadial_explicit_formula`, landed at
`9330ec2f38fe508354301080f8110dc70989458a`
(`Proofs/Theorems/RationalIdeleRadialExplicitFormula.lean:34–49,61–75`;
historical review and replay in `.godel/obligations/rational-explicit-formula.md:1374–1428`).
On actual rational quotient Haar for smooth compact log profiles in the
trivial compact isotype, it evaluates the absolutely summable full-order
open-strip zeta zero sum as the two polar Mellin moments at 0 and 1 minus
the normalized real and finite local terms. This is not an operator trace.
The correspondence to PDF p. 623 (B.4.4.16) remains provisional.

`Masullo.riemannHypothesis_of_strip_test_agreement`
(`Proofs/Props/ZetaTestSumComparison.lean:30–39,77–109` at the pin) is
conditional. It needs arbitrarily small positive half-widths, with one
family of weights at each width, dominated by full zero orders and zero
outside the closed centered strip. Agreement must hold on **every** smooth,
compactly supported positive-log test of integral zero. One width with all
tests yields a strip bound, not RH. Even one diagnostic lacks a common
admissible width with identified geometric operators.

The inherited rendered review in `.godel/obligations/trace-assembly.json`
verifies the final algebra using two evaluations of one integrated Haar
diagonal, not the common diagonal itself (PDF pp. 519–520, Thm. 12.5.1.1,
(12.5.1.1–4)). The inherited review in
`decision-01a20240-39f6-7778-8369-892e00fbbf16.md:42–60` finds an insertion
marker at PDF p. 514 §12.3 and no terminal right projection in the printed
limits at pp. 488–489 (11.2.1.4), (11.2.1.9). The project repair retains it
(`.godel/obligations/trace-hardy-diagonal-q.md:234–257`). The global $S$-piece,
shell lift, and projected right orbital cutoff and tail are not identified
at compatible cutoffs. In particular, the operator alignments at PDF
pp. 494–496 (11.3.1.4), (11.3.2.3), and pp. 517–519 (12.4.1.8–12),
(12.4.1.23–28) remain provisional.

**Reopen RH** after confirming nonduplication and exhibiting one common
admissible positive half-width, with the global $S$-piece, shell lift, and
actual right orbital cutoff specified, retaining the terminal right
projection. Compare for $F=b'$ with a nonzero nonnegative smooth bump $b$
supported in $(0,\log 2)$, taking the level limit before position exhaustion.
Mass and prime samples vanish, but the $+1/2$ Mellin moment is
$-\tfrac12\int e^{x/2}b(x)\,dx\ne0$. Evaluate the actual orbital term rather
than inferring that it vanishes. The unperformed diagnostic is recorded in
`.godel/obligations/trace-to-zeros.md:84–134` and
`.godel/obligations/rational-explicit-formula.md:1430–1444`.

## Full Masullo GRH

The target places every nontrivial Hecke zero on $\Re s=1/2$ for every
continuous unitary idele-class character over every number field, including
ramified characters. Intake independently inspected the rendered wording
in PDF p. 6, Acknowledgements. The rendered scope reading at PDF p. 528
§14.1.2 is inherited from `decision-01a202cd-7acf-7edb-97ce-044d9614d43e.md:78–87`.
The separate simplicity assertion and broader automorphic conjecture are
not this target. The inherited two-reader rendered review in
`.godel/obligations/divisor-heading.json` finds an unresolved reference and
insertion notes, not a divisor theorem, at PDF pp. 522–523 Thm. 13.3.1.2.
The preceding Thm. 13.3.1.1, (13.3.1.1–5), is a capped critical-line sum with
Mellin-jet restrictions, not a full-order comparison.

- The counting-normalized Gaussian radial pairing landed at
  `9e9393019a60e6b275281ca74bd9a11a8f03e0e9`
  (`Proofs/Theorems/IdeleClassGaussianRadial.lean:89–119,178–188`). It equals
  $|\operatorname{disc}K|^{-1/2}$ times the completed Euler product **only
  for $\Re s>1$**, even though the exceptional set covers ramification and
  different support. The accepted scalar norm section at
  `470bd0deacc5d88db570d7531cc7b5c04439fcbf`
  (`Proofs/Theorems/IdeleScalarLogSection.lean:59–99`) gives ordinary
  archimedean dilation $t^{1/[K:\mathbb Q]}$ and finite part 1. The accepted
  fixed-lattice repair at `00d93f83469610f817c3727f8e386b8f7ef278b5`
  (`Proofs/Theorems/IdeleClassGaussianSupport.lean:42–68,73–114`) supplies a
  compact cover of the norm-one classes by actual norm-one ideles and a
  single containing lattice $m^{-1}\mathcal O_K$ for contributing indices.
  This is support control, not a summable archimedean majorant, a large-norm
  decay theorem, or small-norm inversion and zero-mode cancellation after
  norm-one averaging. Historical reviews and replays are recorded in
  `.godel/obligations/single-place-norm.md:835–984`. Intake's rendered check
  at PDF p. 79, Lemma 2.2.2.1(3), confirms the printed nonnegative exponent
  and comparison with $C$; the inverse-ideal support argument is a repair,
  not a verification of that printed bound. Alignment of inversion and
  averaging at PDF pp. 97–98, Thm. 2.2.5.4(2), (4), and pp. 126–127,
  Thm. 3.2.2.1, remains provisional.
- Companion's accepted all-prime factorization at
  `b099fcec178c1f8f53fec179580fb90a410e383d`
  (`Proofs/Theorems/IdealClassEulerFactorization.lean:19–44`) identifies the
  finite Euler product of one finite-unramified class character of
  $\mathbb Q(\sqrt{-5})$ with an entire Dirichlet product on $\Re s>1$.
  This gives continuation of that finite function, not a completed ramified
  contour. The good-height bounds on horizontal segments do not provide
  derivative bounds in their neighborhoods. Historical review and replay
  are attributed in `.godel/obligations/isotypic-euler-factorization-q-sqrt-minus-five.md:622–680`.

For one named ramified test, the completed contour still needs continuation,
conductor and reflection, shifted poles and full-order residues, and
horizontal exhaustion. The existing reflection results assume the requisite
germ or the reflected derivative and integrability
(`Proofs/Props/HeckeContourReflection.lean:28–32,55–70`). Source alignment to
PDF pp. 123–127 (3.2.1.6), Thm. 3.2.2.1, pp. 135–141 §3.3, and pp. 614–615
(B.4.2.1–4) is provisional. Separately, the reviewed width-zero angular
models and pole normalization test do not establish a common normalized
positive-width diagonal. Neither missing input is established for even one
admissible ramified test.

**Reopen full GRH** after confirming nonduplication and supplying both a
completed contour and a normalized common positive-width diagonal for the
same named admissible ramified test. Compare full-order and strip-supported
sums, tracking central and identity terms separately. A rational
specialization or a conditional GRH result does not discharge the target.
