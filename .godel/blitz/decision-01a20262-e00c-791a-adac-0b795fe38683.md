## Classical RH

The target is Mathlib `RiemannHypothesis` for the Riemann zeta function.
`Masullo.rationalIdeleRadial_explicit_formula`
(`Proofs/Theorems/RationalIdeleRadialExplicitFormula.lean:66–75`) is an
unconditional scalar repair on actual rational quotient Haar. It gives
an absolutely summable full-order zero sum, with the two polar Mellin
terms at $s=0,1$ and the real and finite local terms. It is not an
operator trace. `Masullo.riemannHypothesis_of_strip_test_agreement`
(`Proofs/Props/ZetaTestSumComparison.lean:30–39,77–95`) reaches actual
Mathlib RH only under an agreement hypothesis: At each of arbitrarily
small positive half-widths there must be one family of natural weights
bounded by the full orders, vanishing outside the closed centered
strip, whose sum agrees with the full-order sum for **every** smooth,
compactly supported positive-log test of integral zero. The separate
capped-order implication is not this location premise.

The inherited independent rendered review in
`.godel/obligations/trace-assembly.json` verifies the algebra combining
two evaluations of a single Haar diagonal (PDF pp. 519–520,
Thm. 12.5.1.1, (12.5.1.1–4)), not the analytic evaluations. The prior
decision attributes rendered inspection of the insertion marker (PDF
p. 514, §12.3) and the claimed limits without a terminal right
projection (PDF pp. 488–489, (11.2.1.4), (11.2.1.9)) to its evidence
reader. The repair retains that projection at fixed position support
(`.godel/obligations/trace-hardy-diagonal-q.md:234–257`), but has not
identified the actual global $S$-piece and right orbital cutoff. The
geometric split and common Sobolev and edge admissibility remain
unresolved. The relevant interfaces are provisional (PDF pp. 494–496,
(11.3.1.4), (11.3.2.3); pp. 517–519, (12.4.1.8–12), (12.4.1.23–28);
p. 454, (10.3.2.3–4); p. 463, Thm. 10.4.2.8; p. 473, Def. 10.4.3.7).
The rational jet and theta repairs give spectral width models, not
geometric compatibility at those widths.

On the short-bump diagnostic, the derivative of a nonzero nonnegative
smooth bump in $(0,\log 2)$ has zero mass and vanishing prime samples,
but a nonzero $+1/2$ Mellin moment. The joint local projection used as
a substitute gives only the $-1/2$ subtraction on this side of norm
one. Zero prime samples do not imply a zero orbital trace
(`.godel/obligations/trace-to-zeros.md:84–134`). The joint local limit
cannot replace the level-first global comparison.

**Reopen RH** when the actual rational global $S$-piece and right
orbital cutoff are specified with the terminal right projection intact,
and one demonstrably common admissible positive half-width is found.
Then compare both Mellin subtractions and the actual orbital term with
the strip evaluation on that bump derivative, taking the level limit
at fixed position support before exhausting position.

**Reopen full GRH** when a general-character completed contour and a
