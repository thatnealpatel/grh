## Classical RH

The target is Mathlib `RiemannHypothesis` for the Riemann zeta function.
`Masullo.rationalIdeleRadial_explicit_formula`
(`Proofs/Theorems/RationalIdeleRadialExplicitFormula.lean:66`) equates
an absolutely summable full-order zero sum on actual rational quotient
Haar to the two polar Mellin terms at $s=0,1$ minus the real and finite
local terms. This unconditional scalar repair is not an operator trace.
`Masullo.riemannHypothesis_of_strip_test_agreement`
(`Proofs/Props/ZetaTestSumComparison.lean:30,94`) proves RH conditional
on agreement at arbitrarily small common positive half-widths. At each
width one family of natural weights bounded by the full orders and
vanishing outside the closed centered strip must give the full-order
sum on **every** smooth, compactly supported positive-log test of
integral zero. The separate full-versus-capped implication at line 126
is not the location premise.

RH is closer, but there are still no two evaluations of the same global
Haar diagonal giving that agreement. The independent rendered reviews
recorded in `.godel/obligations/trace-assembly.json` confirm the final
algebra (PDF pp. 519–520, Thm. 12.5.1.1, (12.5.1.1–4)), not the
geometric split, orbital vanishing, identification of the $S$-piece,
or spectral diagonal. The cycle's evidence reader directly inspected
the insertion marker (PDF p. 514, §12.3) and the printed convolution
with a left cutoff/projector and no terminal right projector (PDF
pp. 488–489, (11.2.1.4), (11.2.1.9)). The repair retains the right
projection at fixed position support
(`.godel/obligations/trace-hardy-diagonal-q.md:234–257`). Neither the
actual global $S$-piece, shell lift, and right orbital cutoff nor the
comparison of the level-first global limit with the joint local limit
is established. Related assembly readings remain provisional (PDF
pp. 488–496, §§11.2–11.3; pp. 517–519, (12.4.1.8–12),
(12.4.1.23–28)), as do common Sobolev admissibility and the strip-edge
conventions (PDF p. 454, (10.3.2.3–4); p. 463, Thm. 10.4.2.8;
p. 473, Def. 10.4.3.7). The rational jet and theta repairs do not
establish geometry at the widths admitted by the spectral models.

The derivative of a nonzero nonnegative smooth bump in $(0,\log 2)$
has zero mass and vanishing finite prime samples, but a nonzero

projection misses (`.godel/obligations/trace-to-zeros.md:84–134` and
`trace-joint-local-q.md`). Vanishing prime samples does not establish
