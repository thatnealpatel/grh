## Pass 2: LEAN, full-order contour exhaustion for this completion

**Adopted:** `0ec16aa411ec0b6bda96791e666ded761640c801`, based on
`19b36b21138ff26bfa6f4d6916a1c724440d7c30`. The independent writer

`Proofs/Theorems/DirichletThreeResidueSummability.lean:235–288` lands
absolute summability using order transfer, the Jensen bound, and
cubic decay. `Proofs/Theorems/DirichletThreeResidueExhaustion.lean:54–126`
uses the strict strip bound and $T_n\to\infty$ to obtain cofinality and
assemble the limit with the finite residue identity. The rectangle
sums converge for any heights tending to $+\infty$; the contour proof
selects the good heights to eliminate both horizontal terms.

The unconditional theorem
`Masullo.padicRamifiedThree_completed_residue_exhaustion` gives one
sequence of good heights for every smooth compactly supported log
profile $g$. With $C_3$ the exact Mathlib completion, $M_g$ the compact
log Mellin transform, and $V_g(\sigma,t)=M_g(\sigma+it)\,(C_3'/C_3)(\sigma+it)$,
the rectangle sums converge to

$$
\sum_{C_3(\rho)=0} \operatorname{ord}_{\rho}(C_3)\,M_g(\rho)
= \frac{i\int_{\mathbb R}V_g(3/2,t)\,dt -
          i\int_{\mathbb R}V_g(-1/2,t)\,dt}{2\pi i}.
$$

The sum and both vertical integrals converge absolutely. The factors
$i$ are the upward vertical Jacobians, and there are no pole terms for
this entire completion.
