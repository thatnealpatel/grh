## Classical RH

The target is Mathlib `RiemannHypothesis` for the Riemann zeta function.
`Masullo.rationalIdeleRadial_explicit_formula`
(`Proofs/Theorems/RationalIdeleRadialExplicitFormula.lean:66`) evaluates
the absolutely convergent full-order zero sum for smooth compact log
profiles, with both polar moments and the normalized real and finite
local terms. This unconditional scalar formula on normalized rational
idele-class Haar is not a global operator trace.
`Masullo.riemannHypothesis_of_strip_test_agreement`
(`Proofs/Props/ZetaTestSumComparison.lean:30,94`) proves actual RH
**conditional on** one common positive half-width below every positive
bound, with one family of natural weights bounded by the full orders
and vanishing off the corresponding closed strip, such that the full
and weighted sums agree on **every** smooth compact positive-log test
of integral zero. It needs neither a zero-width trace nor a
critical-line multiplicity bound.

RH is the closer target, but the two evaluations of the **same global
Haar diagonal** on common tests at those widths are missing. The
independent rendered review in `.godel/obligations/trace-assembly.json`
verifies the final algebra, not the orbital vanishing, the two Mellin
subtractions from the $S$-piece, the spectral evaluation, or their
common admissibility (PDF pp. 519–520, Thm. 12.5.1.1). The exact shell
lift and right orbital cutoff, local-to-global $S$-piece identification,
strip-edge weights, and Sobolev test compatibility remain provisional
source interfaces (PDF pp. 488–489, (11.2.1.6–10); pp. 492–496,
(11.3.1.2), (11.3.1.4), and §11.3.2; p. 517, (12.4.1.8–12);
pp. 454, 463, and 473, (10.3.2.3–4), Thm. 10.4.2.8, and
Def. 10.4.3.7).

For the derivative of a nonzero nonnegative smooth bump supported in
$(0,\log 2)$, the tested joint local cutoff supplies the $-1/2$ Mellin
subtraction but misses the nonzero $+1/2$ subtraction
(`.godel/obligations/trace-joint-local-q.md` and `trace-to-zeros.md`).
Vanishing linear prime samples does not evaluate the orbitals. The
reviewed ordinary-math Gram calculation in
`.godel/adhoc/rational-theta-gram/handoff.md` retains the terminal right
projection in an unsplit rational model, but neither identifies the
global $S$-piece and orbital cutoff nor controls projection error for
a growing position window. These checks reject specific shortcuts,
not the possibility of a different global repair. Neither reported
loop handoff supplies this comparison, and the pinned proof delta
contains no distinct global operator on which to run it.

**Reopen RH** when a distinct global $S$-piece and right orbital cutoff
are identified with the terminal projection intact. At one common
positive width, take the level limit at fixed position support, then
use the short bump derivative to test both Mellin subtractions and
evaluate the actual orbital contribution. A successful test justifies
further work, not agreement on all tests at arbitrarily small widths.

## Full Masullo GRH

The target is location on $\Re s=1/2$ of every nontrivial Hecke zero for
every number field and every continuous unitary idele-class character.
The prior decision `decision-01a20149-6c55-768f-9fd2-b751564c1bca.md`
records its evidence reader's direct rendered inspection of the scope
and claim (PDF p. 6, Acknowledgements, and p. 528, §14.1.2). This target
is not limited to finite-order characters. The noncentral multiplicity
bound and broader automorphic conjecture are separate (PDF pp. 528–529,
§14.1.2 and Conj. 14.1.2.1). A rational specialization, a single
isotype, or a conditional GRH result cannot discharge it.

The accepted actual-character completed Euler product has nonvanishing,
differentiability, and a split logarithmic derivative only for
$\Re s>1$ (`Proofs/Theorems/CompletedHeckeEulerProduct.lean:98`). The
finite local tests retain the actual Haar unit mass without identifying
the normalized global integral (`FiniteHeckeLocalZeta.lean:66,121`).
Two accepted proof landings materially extend those prerequisites:

- `4e37ced863adec2785cb30724b80b45cdad023ee` establishes the real and
  complex completion-unit Haar transports and measures, and evaluates
  the actual local Gaussian pairings on $\Re s>0$
  (`Proofs/Lemmas/ArchimedeanCompletionHaar.lean` and
  `Proofs/Theorems/ArchimedeanCompletionGaussian.lean:30,64`). It
  retains the two real signs and uses the opposite angular weight in
  the complex test. That weight is a repair to the printed scalar
  pairing, not a verification of the printed covariance (PDF
  pp. 151–153, (3.4.3.1), (3.4.3.5–6), and (3.4.3.12–13);
  inherited rendered review in `complex-gaussian-angular.md`).
- `0515fa9d313e412e61945e3675fff61809d336d8` constructs the open
  number-field idele stage embeddings and proves their cover
  (`Proofs/Theorems/IdeleFiniteStage.lean:78,91,105`). The topology
  does not give the stage Haar restrictions or discriminate between
  measure normalizations. The stage-to-measure manuscript interface
  remains provisional (PDF p. 541, Def. A.1.3.1 and Lem. A.1.3.3,
  and p. 560, (A.3.2.5)).

The general stage Haar and discriminant calibration, normalized global
pairing, actual continuation and reflection with the arithmetic
conductor, zero/pole data and contour bounds and exhaustion, common
global trace, and divisor deduction are still missing in the accepted
chain. The generic reflection interface assumes rather than proves
its reflected identity and integrability inputs. The manuscript
assembly of reflection and contour exhaustion remains provisional
(PDF pp. 123–126, §3.2.2 and Thm. 3.2.2.1; pp. 135–141,
(3.3.1.28–32) and (3.3.2.24); pp. 614–615, (B.4.2.1–4)). The two
independent rendered readers recorded in
`.godel/obligations/divisor-heading.json` found no statement or proof
under Thm. 13.3.1.2 (PDF pp. 522–523). Thm. 13.3.1.1 (PDF p. 522)
uses a capped critical-line sum, not the positive-width spectral sum
of Thm. 12.5.1.1. The mass-zero test does not itself eliminate the
central and identity terms in a prospective divisor comparison.

The bounded upstream checks overlap the main loop's stage Haar work
and the companion's finite normalization audit. The reported
modulus-three Fourier draft (output
`674ae752f7fbcc647c9e93bd55b7dd6f582a838a`, preserved at
`1080bf8534b18b6574115607db70f09046e97d73`) is outside the pinned
ancestry. Its reported local conductor control is not a general
completed contour or a common diagonal, even if the draft were landed.

**Reopen full GRH** when an accepted general-character completed-contour
interface and an explicit common-diagonal or divisor identity permit
a comparison at one positive width in a ramified isotype. Compare full
and strip-supported residue weights on the same admissible test,
accounting for the central and identity terms. That check would
justify further work, not prove the full assertion.
