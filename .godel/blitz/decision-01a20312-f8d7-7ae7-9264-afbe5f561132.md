## Classical RH for the Riemann zeta function

The strongest accepted result is the unconditional scalar theorem
`Masullo.rationalIdeleRadial_explicit_formula`, landing
`9330ec2f38fe508354301080f8110dc70989458a`
(`Proofs/Theorems/RationalIdeleRadialExplicitFormula.lean:34–49,61–75`;
audit `.godel/obligations/rational-explicit-formula.md:1374–1428`). For
smooth compact log profiles on normalized rational idele-class Haar,
the absolutely summable zeta zero sum uses full orders and equals both
polar Mellin terms at $s=0,1$ minus the real and finite local terms. The
real term includes the half-Haar normalization. This is a scalar project
repair, not a Chapter 12 operator trace. Its manuscript alignment is
provisional (PDF p. 614, (B.4.2.2); p. 623, (B.4.4.16); p. 625, (B.4.4.20)).

`Masullo.riemannHypothesis_of_strip_test_agreement`
(`Proofs/Props/ZetaTestSumComparison.lean:30–39,77–109`) gives actual
Mathlib `RiemannHypothesis` **conditional** on test-sum agreement at
arbitrarily small positive half-widths. One weight family per width,
bounded by the true orders and supported on the centered closed strip,
must reproduce the full sum for **every** smooth, compactly supported
positive-log test of integral zero. Agreement for that whole class at
one width gives a strip bound; a single comparison does not.

terms, not either evaluation or a common test domain (PDF pp. 519–520,

same-chain model erases a fixed shell rather than producing the orbital
term (`.godel/obligations/trace-orbital-q.md`, “The right cutoff erases a
fixed shell in the literal chain”). This is an obstruction to that
model, not a verified identification of the manuscript cutoff. The
actual global $S$-piece, shell lift, and right orbital cutoff remain

(11.3.1.4), (11.3.2.3); pp. 517–519, (12.4.1.8–12), (12.4.1.23–28);
