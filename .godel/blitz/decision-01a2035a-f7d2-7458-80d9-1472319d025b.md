## Classical RH for the Riemann zeta function

The accepted unconditional scalar repair
`Masullo.rationalIdeleRadial_explicit_formula` landed at

(`Proofs/Theorems/RationalIdeleRadialExplicitFormula.lean:34–49,61–75`;

the absolutely summable full-order open-strip zeta zero sum is the sum of
the two polar Mellin terms at 0 and 1 minus the real and finite local terms.
The real term already includes the half-Haar normalization. This is not
an operator trace. Source alignment is provisional: PDF p. 614 (B.4.2.2),
p. 619 (B.4.3.13), p. 623 (B.4.4.16), and p. 625 (B.4.4.20), n. 27.
