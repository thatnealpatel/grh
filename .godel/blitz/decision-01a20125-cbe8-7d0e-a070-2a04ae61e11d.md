## Classical RH

The target is actual Mathlib `RiemannHypothesis` for `riemannZeta`. At the
pinned revision, `Masullo.rationalIdeleRadial_explicit_formula`
(`Proofs/Theorems/RationalIdeleRadialExplicitFormula.lean`) unconditionally
evaluates the full-order zeta-zero sum on normalized rational quotient
Haar for smooth compact log profiles, with both polar moments and the real
and finite terms. `Masullo.riemannHypothesis_of_strip_test_agreement`
(`Proofs/Props/ZetaTestSumComparison.lean`) proves actual RH **conditional**
on the following input: For each positive bound on the width, there is a
single positive half-width below it and one collection of natural weights
bounded by full orders and supported in that closed strip, such that the
weighted and full sums agree on **every** smooth compact positive-log test
with integral zero. Neither a width-zero trace nor a multiplicity cap is
required for this endpoint.

What is missing is the geometric evaluation of the same global Haar
diagonal in the required cutoff order. It must identify the global
$S$-piece, retain the terminal right projection, account for both Mellin
subtractions, and control the orbital tail (PDF pp. 488–489,
(11.2.1.6–10); pp. 494–496, (11.3.1.4) and §11.3.2; pp. 519–520,
Thm. 12.5.1.1). The independent rendered review recorded in
`.godel/obligations/trace-assembly.json` verifies the manuscript's final
algebra, not the analytic evaluations or common test conditions. The
transition from the global kernel to the joint local calculation, including
the apparent insertion placeholder on p. 514, §12.3, remains provisional
as a reading and unproved as a comparison. The project’s ordinary-math
rational spectral repairs do not supply this geometry. In the documented
joint local model, a derivative of a nonzero nonnegative bump supported in
$(0,\log 2)$ has zero mass and no linear finite samples, yet the replacement
misses the nonzero $+1/2$ Mellin subtraction. This is a control against that
replacement, not a refutation of every global repair or a bound on the
orbital trace. The main loop already explored collar and cutoff models;
there is no distinct explicit global kernel and cutoff in evidence on which
to repeat the control meaningfully.

**Reopen RH** when an explicit global $S$-piece and orbital cutoff distinct
from those models are available at fixed position support and a common
positive width. Test the actual compressed diagonal on the short mass-zero
bump derivative, checking both Mellin subtractions and the orbital
contribution without inferring the latter from vanishing finite samples.
A successful single comparison would justify further testing, not itself
prove agreement for the full test class at arbitrarily small widths.
