## Classical RH for the Riemann zeta function

`Proofs/Theorems/RationalIdeleRadialExplicitFormula.lean:34–75` gives
an unconditional, absolutely convergent full-order open-strip zero sum
on normalized rational idele-class Haar, with two polar Mellin moments
and normalized real and finite terms. Its mapping to PDF p. 623,
(B.4.4.16), is provisional and includes a normalization repair.
`Proofs/Theorems/RationalFixedAnnulusTrace.lean:21–41` gives a
basis-independent local fixed-annulus trace retaining the right
projection. The reviewed ordinary-math repairs in
`.godel/obligations/trace-spectral-strip-q.md` and `trace-theta-density-q.md`
give rational trivial-isotype spectral inputs at almost every positive
half-width, not the geometric evaluation.

The new Lean result at `76f28c1a9593ff461f8e261cae990874d53d4771`
(`RationalOrbitalSectionWeight.lean:104–152`,
`RationalOrbitalPrimeTwo.lean:199–215`, and
`RationalOrbitalObstruction.lean:25–35`) shows that the literal double
section weight under upstairs rational norm-one Haar is zero for every
pair. No kernel with that weight gives the unshifted prime-two right
side, $\log 2$. The previous Blitz decision and
`.godel/obligations/trace-geometric.md:45–64` attest the printed factors
and missing inner differential at PDF p. 495, (11.3.2.1–3), not the
intended measure. The same-chain right mask independently erases a
fixed shell (`trace-orbital-q.md:241–379`). These obstructions do not
evaluate the actual orbital trace. The negative short-test result for
the normalized collar (`trace-section-collar-q.md:71–158`) concerns a
substitute operator, not the required global comparison.

The missing identification is the actual global $S=\{\infty\}$ diagonal
in the decomposition at PDF p. 494, (11.3.1.4), with the local limit at
pp. 517–519, (12.4.1.8–12) and (12.4.1.23–28). This mapping is
provisional. The two-reader rendered record
`.godel/obligations/trace-assembly.json` verifies the final algebra at
pp. 519–520, Thm. 12.5.1.1, not the analytic evaluations with both Mellin
subtractions.

**Reopen RH** after confirming nonduplication and identifying a
Haar-compatible lift of the actual global kernel with a shell-preserving
right projection and compatible cutoffs at one admissible positive
half-width. Run the control in `.godel/obligations/trace-to-zeros.md:84–142`:
Use the derivative of a nonzero nonnegative smooth bump supported in
$(0,\log 2)$. Take the level limit before position exhaustion, and
compute the actual orbital contribution separately from the nonzero
$+1/2$ Mellin moment. Vanishing finite linear samples are not an orbital
evaluation. A successful control would not discharge
`Proofs/Props/ZetaTestSumComparison.lean:30–39,77–109`, which requires
agreement on every smooth compact positive-log mass-zero test for one
order-dominated strip-supported weight family per width, at arbitrarily
small positive widths.

## Full Masullo GRH

The target is $\Re s=1/2$ for every nontrivial Hecke zero of every
continuous unitary idele-class character over every number field,
including ramified characters. The scope is attributed to the rendered
reading of PDF p. 6, Acknowledgements, and p. 528, §14.1.2, in
`decision-01a202cd-7acf-7edb-97ce-044d9614d43e.md:78–87`. Multiplicity
and higher-rank claims are separate. The two-reader rendered review
`.godel/obligations/divisor-heading.json` finds no divisor statement at
PDF pp. 522–523, Thm. 13.3.1.2. The preceding theorem's restricted
Mellin-jet tests and capped orders, (13.3.1.1–3), are not full-order
agreement.

`Proofs/Theorems/FiniteBoxPoisson.lean:49–59`, landed at
`f40f070bd2aea23b3d0b2879d81f7314929f4cde`, equates absolutely convergent
translated-box diagonal sums for every number field, including both
zero indices, using actual local Fourier integrals. Accepted stage Haar
at `5ebb1863c3039c8c8d54c428f6624b4bae43a788` and global additive Haar at
`c363549d11af7aa056a75a8ab0b71160a60bdf49` give compatible stage products
and unique regular additive Haar retaining exceptional masses. The
scoped check handoff is `.godel/obligations/single-place-norm.md:1584–1746`.
This does not identify the global Fourier integral, establish the
counting-quotient normalization, or give ramified inversion. The source
mappings to PDF p. 82, Thm. 2.2.3.2, and pp. 555–558, §A.3.1, are
provisional.

`PadicRamifiedThreeFourier.lean:35–128`, `DirichletContourReflection.lean:24–70`,
and `PadicConductorZeta.lean:218–264` supply a local transform, a vertical
Dirichlet reflection with conductor contact, and primitive prime-power
local Haar integrals. The last landed at
`8389af5c00756c25ec1c2979daec553835059c58`. The conductor-three ratio has
magnitude $\sqrt{3}$, contrary to a constant finite factor under the
inherited reading of PDF p. 136, (3.3.1.30), and consistent with the
variation at p. 141, (3.3.2.24)
(`.godel/obligations/hecke-right-line-majorant.md:622–687`). This is not
a phase identification or a global functional equation. Neither the
ramified local checks nor the rational specialization fills the gap.

**Reopen full GRH** after confirming nonduplication and identifying both
sides for the same odd quadratic character of conductor 3: A completed
idelic contour with justified horizontal exhaustion and full-order
residues, and a normalized common positive-width diagonal with separate
conductor, identity, pole, and central-zero accounting. One match would
not establish full GRH. A mismatch would reject the proposed comparison,
not either hypothesis.
