## Classical RH for the Riemann zeta function

The strongest accepted result is the unconditional scalar project repair
`Masullo.rationalIdeleRadial_explicit_formula` at
`9330ec2f38fe508354301080f8110dc70989458a`
(`Proofs/Theorems/RationalIdeleRadialExplicitFormula.lean:34–49,61–75`;
historical checks in `.godel/obligations/rational-explicit-formula.md:1374–1428`).

the absolutely summable full-order sum over the open-strip zeta zeros equals

The real term already incorporates the half-Haar normalization. This is not
an operator trace. Manuscript alignment is provisional: PDF p. 614 (B.4.2.2),
p. 623 (B.4.4.16), and p. 625 (B.4.4.20).

`Masullo.riemannHypothesis_of_strip_test_agreement`
(`Proofs/Props/ZetaTestSumComparison.lean:30–39,77–109`) concludes actual

half-widths, a single weight family per width, dominated by the true orders
and zero off the centered closed strip, must reproduce the full-order sum
on **every** smooth, compactly supported positive-log test of integral zero.
One width with agreement for the whole test class would give a strip bound,
not RH. One diagnostic test is weaker still.
