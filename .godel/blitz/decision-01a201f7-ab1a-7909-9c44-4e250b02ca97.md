## Classical RH

The target is actual Mathlib `RiemannHypothesis` for the Riemann zeta
function. `Masullo.rationalIdeleRadial_explicit_formula`
(`Proofs/Theorems/RationalIdeleRadialExplicitFormula.lean:66`) gives
an unconditional scalar identity on normalized rational idele-class Haar:
The absolutely summable full-order zero sum equals the two polar Mellin
terms at $s=0,1$ minus the normalized real and finite local terms. This
is a project contour and normalization repair, not an operator trace.
`Masullo.riemannHypothesis_of_strip_test_agreement`
(`Proofs/Props/ZetaTestSumComparison.lean:30,94`) gives RH only if below
every positive bound there is a common positive half-width and a single
family of natural weights bounded by full orders and supported in the
closed strip, with equality to the full-order sum on **every** smooth
compact positive-log test of integral zero. It does not need an equality
at width zero or a multiplicity cap.

RH is the closer target. The missing input is the pair of analytic
evaluations of the **same global Haar diagonal** on common tests at
common widths. The independent rendered reviews in
`.godel/obligations/trace-assembly.json` verify the final algebra (PDF
pp. 519–520, Thm. 12.5.1.1, (12.5.1.1–4)), not the invoked geometric
split, orbital vanishing, $S$-piece evaluation with both subtractions,
or strip evaluation. The independently rendered finding recorded in
`decision-01a201d4-11f4-7985-b299-2ad2cc1b87bc.md` is that PDF p. 514,
§12.3 contains a heading and insertion marker, not a global-to-local
diagonal analysis. Still provisional are the exact shell lift and
right orbital cutoff, the terminal right projection and level-first
limit, the identification of the separate joint local cutoff with the
actual global $S$-piece (PDF pp. 488–489, (11.2.1.4), (11.2.1.6–10);
pp. 492–496, (11.3.1.2–4), (11.3.2.2–5); pp. 517–519, (12.4.1.8–12),
(12.4.1.23–25)), and the strip-edge weights and shared Sobolev test
admissibility (PDF pp. 454, 463, and 473, (10.3.2.3–4),
Thm. 10.4.2.8, and Def. 10.4.3.7).

The joint local replacement supplies the $-1/2$ subtraction but misses
the nonzero $+1/2$ one on the derivative of a nonzero nonnegative smooth
bump supported in $(0,\log 2)$ (`.godel/obligations/trace-to-zeros.md`,
lines 84–134, and `trace-joint-local-q.md` in the same directory).
Vanishing finite linear samples do not evaluate the orbitals. The
unsplit fixed-grid Gram model preserves the right projection but
identifies neither cutoff (`.godel/adhoc/rational-theta-gram/handoff.md`).
The companion's independently rendered real-prime audit, attributed in
`.godel/obligations/trace-real-prime-q.md:136–252`, distinguishes $R/2$
in the scalar limit from $R/4$ for the defined prime with the exterior
half, where $R$ is the audit's full-Haar integral (PDF pp. 518–519,
(12.4.1.24–25), and pp. 619–620, (B.4.3.13), (B.4.4.1–2)). Substituting
the printed kernel recovers $R/2$ but does not evaluate the defined
prime. Neither diagnostic is a global trace evaluation, and rerunning
either would not test the missing comparison.

**Reopen RH** when the actual rational global $S$-piece and right
orbital cutoff are specified with the terminal right projection intact
and a common admissible positive half-width is identified. Then compare
both Mellin subtractions and the actual orbital contribution against
the strip evaluation on the short bump derivative, taking the level
limit at fixed position support before exhausting position. Agreement
on that one test would justify further work, not prove RH.

## Full Masullo GRH

The target is location on $\Re s=1/2$ of every nontrivial Hecke zero for
every number field and every continuous unitary idele-class character.
The independent rendered scope review is recorded in
`decision-01a20149-6c55-768f-9fd2-b751564c1bca.md` (PDF p. 6,
Acknowledgements, and p. 528, §14.1.2). The noncentral multiplicity
bound and the broader automorphic conjecture are separate (PDF
pp. 528–529, §14.1.2 and Conj. 14.1.2.1). Neither a rational or
single-isotype specialization nor a conditional result discharges GRH.

The pinned completed Euler product is nonvanishing and differentiable
with a split logarithmic derivative on $\Re s>1$
(`Proofs/Theorems/CompletedHeckeEulerProduct.lean:98`); this result
supplies no meromorphic continuation. The grouped stage formula,
outside the previous decision's pin but inside this one, is
`Masullo.ideleFiniteStage_zetaIntegral` at
`a43a6002ba70684df26e66c1972a94a8a9083abe`
(`Proofs/Theorems/IdeleFiniteStageIntegral.lean:102`). The substantive
new results, checked as declarations at their exact landings, are:

- `Masullo.ideleZetaIntegral_rightHalfPlane` at
  `04e0fc6889d4bfc5d3d70ea464fffb8cd4da3ffa`
  (`Proofs/Theorems/IdeleZetaIntegral.lean:67`) exhausts the raw upstairs
  complex and absolute integrals on $\Re s>1$. It assumes exact stage
  comaps, unit mass one and unramifiedness off a finite set, and local
  integrability of the grouped Archimedean and exceptional terms. The
  exceptional factors remain in the products.
- `Masullo.ideleHeckeGaussian_zetaIntegral` at
  `e55aab27b744bfb62a4aaa57ea9f13818aad1055`
  (`Proofs/Theorems/IdeleHeckeGaussian.lean:104,177`) uses a fixed
  corrected Gaussian with opposite complex angular weight. It proves
  absolute integrability and the Euler value times actual finite unit
  masses on $\Re s>1$, still with supplied exact stage comaps.
- `Masullo.prod_differentIdeal_unit_scales` at
  `5563a07927b65c8eca3ca6f2dab255ed0491bde3`
  (`Proofs/Theorems/DifferentUnitScales.lean:23,31,51`) assembles the
  finitely supported different exponents into the absolute discriminant
  and the inverse square-root scale. It does not identify the
  actual Haar mass at each place.
- The primitive conductor results at
  `cb82fb5e43b72408d3867792d55e2ca7767c2c83`
  (`Proofs/Theorems/PadicConductorFourier.lean:142,179,193`) give a
  finite Fourier reduction, the exact shell $|p^ny|_p=1$ via
  `Masullo.padicConductorFourier_ne_zero_iff`, and unit covariance for
  primitive prime-power characters over $\mathbb Q_p$.
  This is not a finite-extension theorem or a global reflection.

These are project repairs and prerequisites, not verification of the
printed identity. Historical statement and replay locators for the
right-half-plane results are in
`.godel/obligations/hecke-right-line-majorant.md:1220–1382`. The locally
reviewed two-ball residue diagnostic in
`.godel/obligations/finite-local-zeta-residue.md` is informal: Identical
$\widehat f(0)=1/3$ gives equal printed residues, while the defining

p. 141, (3.3.2.23–25), and p. 560, (A.3.2.2–3), independently rendered
by the reviewer attributed in that note). That check is not the full
multiplicative test-domain inclusion (PDF pp. 259–260, (5.3.4.1),
Lem. 5.3.4.2, provisional).
