## Classical RH

The target is actual Mathlib `RiemannHypothesis` for the Riemann zeta
function. At the pin, `Masullo.rationalIdeleRadial_explicit_formula`
(`Proofs/Theorems/RationalIdeleRadialExplicitFormula.lean:66`) gives the
unconditional scalar formula on normalized rational idele-class Haar:
The absolutely summable full-order zero sum equals the two polar Mellin
terms minus the normalized real and finite local terms. This is a
checked rational formula, not a global operator trace or certification
of the printed normalization. The pinned
`Masullo.riemannHypothesis_of_strip_test_agreement`
(`Proofs/Props/ZetaTestSumComparison.lean:30,94`) reaches RH provided
that below every positive half-width bound there is one common positive
half-width and one family of natural weights bounded by full orders and
supported in its closed strip, with agreement against the full sum on
**every** smooth compact positive-log test of integral zero. Neither a
zero-width trace nor a multiplicity cap is required.

RH is the closer target, but the two analytic evaluations of the **same
global Haar diagonal** on common tests and at common widths are missing.
The independent rendered reviews recorded in
`.godel/obligations/trace-assembly.json` verify the final algebra in
Thm. 12.5.1.1, PDF pp. 519–520, (12.5.1.1–4), not the asserted geometric
and spectral evaluations. The cycle evidence reader independently
inspected rendered p. 514, §12.3 and n. 1, and found a heading and
insertion marker, not a local-diagonal analysis. Decisive interfaces
remain provisional: The shell lift/right orbital cutoff, level-first
operator with the terminal right projection, and identification of the
separate local cutoff with the actual global $S$-piece (PDF pp. 488–489,
(11.2.1.4), (11.2.1.6–10); pp. 492–496, (11.3.1.2–4), (11.3.2.2–5);
pp. 517–519, (12.4.1.8–12), (12.4.1.23–25)), and the strip-edge weights
and shared Sobolev test admissibility (PDF pp. 454, 463, and 473,
(10.3.2.3–4), Thm. 10.4.2.8, and Def. 10.4.3.7).

The tested joint local replacement retains the $-1/2$ moment but
misses the nonzero $+1/2$ subtraction on the derivative of a nonzero
nonnegative smooth bump supported in $(0,\log 2)$
(`.godel/obligations/trace-joint-local-q.md`). Its vanishing linear
prime samples do not evaluate the orbitals. The reviewed unsplit Gram
model (`.godel/adhoc/rational-theta-gram/handoff.md`) retains the terminal
projection at fixed grid but identifies neither actual cutoff. At the
new pin, `.godel/obligations/trace-real-prime-q.md` records rendered
checks of PDF pp. 518–519, (12.4.1.24–25), and pp. 619–620,
(B.4.3.13), (B.4.4.1–2). On its short two-bump control the informal
scalar limit is $R/2$ for the note's full-Haar integral $R$. The next
display gives $R/4$ with the defined prime and its exterior half, and
$R/2$ if the printed kernel is substituted instead. This documents a
local mismatch, not a Lean trace theorem or an evaluation of the
actual $S$-diagonal. Rerunning either local diagnostic would not test
the missing same-diagonal comparison.

**Reopen RH** when the actual rational global $S$-piece and right
orbital cutoff are specified with the terminal right projection intact
and a common admissible positive half-width is identified. At that
width, take the level limit at fixed position support before exhausting
position. Use the short bump derivative to check both Mellin
subtractions and the actual orbital contribution against the strip
evaluation. Success on one test would justify further work, not prove
agreement for all required tests and widths.

## Full Masullo GRH

The target is location on $\Re s=1/2$ of every nontrivial Hecke zero for
every number field and every continuous unitary idele-class character.
The prior independent scope reading is recorded in
`decision-01a20149-6c55-768f-9fd2-b751564c1bca.md` (PDF p. 6,

  and raw zeta-density factorizations at
  `9ccc21cec801b5db226d8e415666f77e9aa36d33`
