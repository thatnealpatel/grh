## Pass 1 (INFORMAL): Conductor shell, amplitude, and Mellin comparison

Let $F=K_v$ be an arbitrary ramified finite completion for the given
unitary character. Write $\theta=\chi_v$, $\mathcal O$ for the integer
ring, $U=\mathcal O^\times$, $\pi$ for a uniformizer, $q$ for the
residue cardinal, $d$ for the different exponent, and $n\ge1$ for the
least positive index with $\theta|_{1+\pi^n\mathcal O}=1$. Existence
uses the principal-unit kernel neighborhood and neighborhood base
(`Lemmas/FiniteHeckePrincipalUnits.lean:62–67`); ramification means
$\theta|_U\ne1$. Put $k=d+n$ and $c=\pi^{-k}$.

The actual finite additive test is $f=\theta^{-1}1_U$
(`FiniteAdditiveHeckeGaussian.lean:36–51`). Its restriction, its position
in the tensor, and the inclusion of all ramified places follow from
`AdeleHeckeTest.lean:42–74`, `AdeleHeckeGaussianTheta.lean:70–90`, and
`IdeleClassGaussianContinuation.lean:70–102`. At this place the stage
Haar supplies the normalized additive measure $\nu$
(`AdeleFiniteStageHaar.lean:30–37`). The local Fourier value in the
adelic transform is
$$H(y)=\int_F f(x)\psi_v(xy)\,d\nu(x),\qquad
\psi_v=\operatorname{padicStdAddChar}\circ\operatorname{Tr}_{F/\mathbb Q_p}.$$
The finite trace phase is **positive**: The outer conjugation in
`Lemmas/AdeleFourier.lean:17–19` cancels the finite conjugation in
`AdeleTraceCharacter.lean:27–32` and
`FiniteAdeleTraceCharacter.lean:57–67,101–106`. The box factorization
at actual frequencies is `AdeleBoxFourier.lean:46–55` and
`FiniteBoxFourier.lean:35–57`. The separately named local transform in
`FiniteCompletionFourierSquare.lean:25–27` has the opposite phase and
must not be used to fix the amplitude of this transform.

**Fourier support and nonvanishing.** The annihilator of $\mathcal O$
for $\psi_v$ is $\pi^{-d}\mathcal O$
(`FiniteCompletionIntegersFourier.lean:33–48`,
`Lemmas/FiniteCompletionCodifferentFourier.lean:26–58`, and
`FiniteCompletionDifferentScale.lean:39–50`). The annihilator of
$\pi^n\mathcal O$ is therefore $c\mathcal O$. Expanding $f$ on its
constant unit cosets modulo $\pi^n\mathcal O$, the translated-ball
formula (`Lemmas/FiniteCompletionBoxIntegral.lean:21–28`) gives zero
off $c\mathcal O$. On $c\mathcal O$, with $T=\mathcal O/\pi^n\mathcal O$,
it gives
$$H(cb)=hS(b),\qquad
S(b)=\sum_{a\in T^\times}\theta(a)^{-1}\psi_v(cab),\qquad
h=\nu(\pi^n\mathcal O)=q^{-n-d/2}.$$
The mass follows from the normalized integer mass, additive dilation,
and the prime-power quotient (`Lemmas/FiniteCompletionNormalizedHaar.lean:143–165`,
`Lemmas/FiniteCompletionPrimePow.lean:146–159`). For a nonunit $b$ and
$n>1$, choose $u\in1+\pi^{n-1}\mathcal O$ with $\theta(u)\ne1$.
Reindexing by $a\mapsto au$ changes the character weight and not the
phase, since $ab(u-1)\in\pi^n\mathcal O$. For $n=1$ a nonunit $b$
is zero in $T$, and one uses any $u\in U$ with $\theta(u)\ne1$. Thus
the inner frequencies, including zero, vanish. For unit $b$,
reindexing by $a\mapsto ab$ gives $S(b)=\theta(b)S(1)$.

The pairing $(a,b)\mapsto\psi_v(cab)$ on $T$ is perfect: Triviality in
$b$ means $ca\in\pi^{-d}\mathcal O$, or $a=0$ in $T$. Additive
orthogonality and unitarity give
$\sum_{b\in T}|S(b)|^2=|T|\,|T^\times|$. By the support and
covariance, $|S(1)|^2=|T|=q^n$. Hence, for $A=H(c)$,
$$|A|=q^{-k/2}>0,\qquad H(cu)=A\theta(u)\quad(u\in U).$$
The support is exactly $cU$. The profile is $A$ times the actual dual
finite Gaussian evaluated at $c^{-1}y$, not the undilated Gaussian.
Character conjugation commutes with restriction and preserves
ramification, and unitarity identifies conjugation with inversion
(`Lemmas/HeckeCharacterConjugate.lean:17–47`,
`Lemmas/FiniteHeckeLocalNormalization.lean:17–41`).

**Mellin comparison.** Let $m=\mu^\times(U)=q^{-d/2}>0$ be the actual
multiplicative Haar mass
(`FiniteCompletionMultiplicativeHaar.lean:45–73`,
`FiniteCompletionDifferentExtension.lean:69–82`). It is not the mass
of an additive conductor ball. The forward weight is
$\theta(x)|x|^s$, from `IdeleHeckeGaussian.lean:97–140` and
`IdeleClassGaussianRadial.lean:154–161,182–189`. Under reciprocal
substitution in the pointwise theta inversion the norm prefactor
$|a|^{-1}$ sends the global weight to $\chi(b)^{-1}|b|^{1-s}$ for
$b=a^{-1}$. This identifies the local reflected twist without a
claim about a global integral interchange. The forward integrand is
$1_U$. On $cU$ the reflected integrand at $cu$ is
$A\theta(c)^{-1}q^{k(1-s)}$: The unit twists cancel, $|c|=q^k$,
and multiplying by $c$ preserves multiplicative Haar. Both integrals
converge for all $s$, with values $m$ and
$mA\theta(c)^{-1}q^{k(1-s)}$. The forward-over-reflected quotient is
$$R(s)=\theta(c)A^{-1}q^{k(s-1)},\qquad
R(s)/R(1/2)=q^{k(s-1/2)}.$$
Changing the representative $c$ to $cu_0$ with $u_0\in U$ multiplies
$A$ and $\theta(c)$ by the same factor. No condition $\theta(\pi)=1$
is used.
