## Classical RH for the Riemann zeta function

The accepted repair `Masullo.rationalIdeleRadial_explicit_formula`
(`9330ec2f38fe508354301080f8110dc70989458a`,
`Proofs/Theorems/RationalIdeleRadialExplicitFormula.lean:34–75`) gives an
unconditional scalar formula on actual rational quotient Haar. For
smooth compact radial log profiles, the absolutely summable full-order
open-strip zeta zero sum equals the two polar Mellin moments at $0$ and
$1$ minus the normalized local terms. It is not an operator trace.
Alignment with PDF p. 623, (B.4.4.16), remains provisional.

continuous unitary idele-class character over every number field,
including ramified characters. This scope, distinct from multiplicity
bounds and the broader automorphic conjecture, is attributed to the
rendered review in `decision-01a202cd-7acf-7edb-97ce-044d9614d43e.md:78–87`
of PDF p. 6, Acknowledgements, and p. 528, §14.1.2. The inherited
two-reader rendered review in `.godel/obligations/divisor-heading.json`
finds insertion notes, not a statement of Thm. 13.3.1.2, at PDF
pp. 522–523. Thm. 13.3.1.1 gives a restricted capped sum, not full-order
equality.

- The corrected Gaussian has uniform absolute orbit-sum decay at norm
  at least one (`Proofs/Theorems/IdeleClassGaussianRapidDecay.lean:48–56`).
  The accepted slice result at `0abc265a52c15782f83bab34e30fc0ec14b8b70a`
  gives integrability at each nonnegative log radius with a bound
  using the exact quotient mass. On the same witnesses the radial
  Mellin identity gives the discriminant-scaled completed Euler
  product only for $\Re s>1$
  (`Proofs/Theorems/IdeleClassGaussianRadial.lean:96–105,150–180`).
  Small-norm inversion still needs both zero modes, with cancellation
  for a nontrivial norm-one restriction only after averaging. The
  source reading remains provisional at PDF p. 82, (2.2.3.2),
  pp. 97–98, Thm. 2.2.5.4, and pp. 126–127, Thm. 3.2.2.1.
- `aecf6de8fc2ef566569bceaf15e175d62a320fba` gives reciprocal bounds
  for every derivative order at one common good height across the
  closed horizontal segment for the explicit product of one
  finite-unramified class character over $\mathbb Q(\sqrt{-5})$
  (`Proofs/Theorems/IdealClassGoodHeightDerivatives.lean:20–25`). The
  denominator is a repair per the inherited rendered reading of PDF
  pp. 308–309, (6.3.2.16–17), recorded in
  `.godel/obligations/isotypic-inverse-range-q-sqrt-minus-five.md`.
  Numerator decay through the endpoints is unverified; the test-space
  reading at PDF pp. 800–801, Def. D.3.1.3, is provisional.
- The primitive Dirichlet conductor reflection is a partial input;
  the general reflection interfaces assume a functional-equation
  germ or a reflected logarithmic derivative and right-line
  integrability (`Proofs/Props/HeckeContourReflection.lean:28–32,55–62`).
  There is no checked completed contour paired with a normalized

  reviewed non-Lean raw-Haar pole test over $\mathbb Q(\sqrt{-5})$
  (`.godel/obligations/trace-window-q-sqrt-minus-five.md`, PDF

**Reopen full GRH** after confirming nonduplication and obtaining both
an identified completed contour, with conductor reflection, poles,
full-order residues, and horizontal exhaustion, and a normalized

Compare full-order and strip-supported sums, accounting separately
for conductor and identity terms, any poles, and central zeros. A
rational specialization or a conditional GRH result is not full GRH.
